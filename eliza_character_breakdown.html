Character Overview

Real Life

  • Full Name: Eliza Zhao
  • Age: High school senior (couple years younger than Jason)
  • Personality: INFP, Enneagram 9w1, True Neutral. Conflict-avoidant, passive, but possesses a strong inner moral core that emerges under pressure.
  • Background: Her parents are relentlessly pushy, forcing her into constant extra courses to prepare for college and medical school. She feels her entire life has been planned out — and she doesn't actually want to be a doctor. She's burned out and apathetic.
  • Escape into AO: Fearing the pressure will destroy her, her parents allow her to play Awaken Online as a brief respite. She has never really played many games before, making her a discovery-story character who experiences AO without gamer preconceptions.
  • Initial desire: She starts the game wanting to tend to a virtual garden and tinker with herbs — a quiet, non-combat escape.

In Game

  • In-Game Name: Eliza (same)
  • Class Path: Water Mage → Alchemist → Water Avatar
  • Race: Human
  • Affinity: Water — Acceptance
  • Alignment: True Neutral
  • Faction: Original Sin / Twilight Throne
  • God: Avatar of "the Hippie" (Water God)
  • Titles: The Ice Witch, Avatar of Water, Regent of Sea's Edge (formerly Falcon's Hook), The Farm Girl
  • Reputation: Known among the player base as the "Ice Witch" — a stark contrast to her real-life meek personality. Has a surprisingly high player kill count.

The Transformation

Eliza's reluctant journey from an apathetic student to the feared Ice Witch of Awaken Online
Apathy (Book 3.5)Novice → Water Mage

Reluctant Awakening

Eliza logs into AO for the first time seeking peace — a virtual garden to tend and herbs to tinker with. Instead, she encounters the Water Affinity God (nicknamed "the Hippie"), a capricious, annoying figure accompanied by his loyal black sheep Fluffy. The Hippie forces her to become a water mage and makes her his avatar against her will, sending her on a series of bizarre quests around the city of Falcon's Hook.

Apathy (Book 3.5)Water Mage → Alchemist

Discovery of Alchemy

Eliza discovers her niche: alchemy and herbalism. Using her real-world knowledge of biology and horticulture, she excels at potion-crafting. She learns that potions require precise ingredients, long training, and deep knowledge of plants — they don't appear like in traditional MMORPGs. Potions can also cause side effects if used carelessly. She begins blending her water magic with alchemical powders, creating a unique combat style centered around her signature Obscuring Mist.

Apathy (Book 3.5)Alchemist → Weapon of Mass Destruction

The Farm — A Zone-Destroying Weapon

In one of the most remarkable feats in the series, Eliza creates a weapon capable of destroying an entire zone — and potentially all of Awaken Online itself. This "farm thing" is considered by some readers to be potentially superior to even Jason's dark powers in scale of destruction. Despite her meek, shy, conflict-avoidant nature, she racks up a significant number of player kills, earning her the reputation as the "Ice Witch" among the player base.

Evolution (Book 3)Joining Original Sin

Meeting Jason Rhodes

Eliza eventually meets Jason Rhodes and his companions and decides to join the Twilight Throne and the guild Original Sin. She becomes a trusted ally and is appointed as regent of Falcon's Hook (later renamed "Sea's Edge"), governing the city on behalf of the Twilight Throne. Her administrative skills and alchemical knowledge prove invaluable.

Armageddon (Book 6)Avatar of Water — Full Power

The Ice Witch Unleashed

During the avatar trials in Armageddon, Eliza demonstrates her full power as the Water Avatar. She nearly wipes out the infamous PvP guild Death and Taxes single-handedly using her water magic and alchemical enhancements. The Hippie gives her a bell that grants her remote access to the water mana well in Sea's Edge — a reward for finally standing up to him.

Hellion (Book 5) → Crucible (Book 8)Key Ally

Strategic Role in the Avatar Wars

Eliza participates alongside the other avatars (Jason, Finn, Alexion) and their lieutenants in high-stakes strategic gameplay. She proves herself as a master of area control and alchemical warfare. Her mist-based combat style and potion mastery make her a unique and deadly force on the battlefield — a quiet girl who can flood a zone with poisonous mist and freeze her enemies solid before they even see her.

Powers & Abilities

A comprehensive catalog of Eliza's arsenal across the series

Water Affinity Magic

As the Avatar of the Water God, Eliza wields the element of Water — associated with the concept of Acceptance. She commands water in all its forms: liquid, vapor (mist), and solid (ice). Her spells scale with her connection to the water mana well and her alchemical enhancements. She learns to combine spells creatively rather than relying on raw power.

Water Manipulation Ice Magic Mist Control

Obscuring Mist (Signature Skill)

Eliza's signature ability. She generates a dense magical mist that blankets an area, obscuring vision and disorienting enemies. What makes this truly deadly is her ability to inject powdered potions into the mist, creating a versatile area-control toolkit that can deploy buffs, healing, poisons, or debuffs depending on what she mixes in.

Mist Generation Potion Infusion Area Control

Alchemy & Herbalism

Eliza's true specialty. She combines real-world knowledge of biology and horticulture with in-game alchemy systems to create potent potions. She grows her own ingredients using water magic to accelerate plant growth. Her potions range from healing concoctions to deadly poisons. She understands that potions have side effects if used carelessly — a depth of mechanics most players ignore.

Plant Growth Potion Crafting Poison Making

Ice Traps & Frost Weaving

Eliza creates ice traps laced with poisons, freezing enemies in place while the toxins do their work. She can weave ice into barriers, projectiles, and complex formations. Her frost magic is typically used defensively or for area denial, creating killing fields within her mist.

Ice Traps Frost Barriers Poison Lacing

Mana Well (Avatar Ability)

As an Avatar of Water, Eliza has access to a mana well — a deep reservoir of water-attuned mana that allows her to perform extraordinary feats beyond normal players. After standing up to the Hippie, she receives a bell that grants her remote access to the water mana well in Sea's Edge, allowing her to draw on it even when far from the city.

Mana Well Remote Access Bell Avatar Scaling

The Farm — Zone-Destroying Weapon

Eliza's most infamous creation. She constructs a "farm" that serves as a weapon capable of destroying an entire zone — and potentially all of Awaken Online. The exact mechanics are tied to her alchemical mastery combined with water magic. Some fans consider this feat potentially superior to even Jason's dark powers in terms of raw destructive scale. The weapon demonstrates that Eliza is far more dangerous than she appears.

Zone Destruction WMD Unique Creation

Character Progression

Stat analysis and growth trajectory across the series

Build Philosophy

Eliza is a control mage and alchemist. Unlike traditional water mages who focus on direct damage, she invests heavily in Intelligence (for alchemy and potion potency) and Wisdom (for mana regeneration and sustained mist upkeep). Her Charisma is surprisingly high due to her relationship with the Hippie, and her avatar status gives her unusual stats for a non-combat-focused player.

Growth & Player Kills

Despite starting as a non-combat player, Eliza accumulates a significant number of player kills — earning her the "Ice Witch" moniker. Her kill count surprises even veteran players. Her power scales dramatically once she gains remote access to the Sea's Edge mana well, allowing her to sustain her mist-based combat style indefinitely. Her alchemical knowledge makes her exponentially more dangerous: stronger ingredients = stronger potions = stronger mist.

Enemies & Rivals

Those who have crossed the Ice Witch

Death and Taxes PvP Guild

An infamous PvP guild hired by Jason's enemies. They become a recurring threat to the Twilight Throne. In Armageddon (Book 6), Eliza nearly wipes them out single-handedly using her enhanced avatar abilities and alchemical mist, cementing her reputation as the Ice Witch.

The Hippie Complicated

While technically her god and patron, the Hippie is more antagonist than ally. He wastes her time, gives her absurd quests, and makes her life difficult. Their relationship is defined by mutual exasperation. Eliza eventually earns his respect by standing up to him.

Player Killers & PK Guilds

Eliza accumulates a significant player kill count through self-defense and strategic engagements. The player base comes to fear the "Ice Witch" — a quiet girl who can flood a zone with poisonous mist and leave nothing but frozen corpses.

Key Artifacts & Items

Powerful objects in Eliza's possession

Remote Mana Well Bell

Given by the Hippie after Eliza finally stands up to him. This bell grants her remote access to the water mana well in Sea's Edge (formerly Falcon's Hook). Allows her to draw on the city's mana reserves from anywhere in AO — a dramatic power boost to her sustained combat and mist generation.

The Farm (WMD)

Eliza's signature creation. A specialized alchemical-water apparatus that functions as a weapon of mass destruction capable of destroying an entire zone. Combines her alchemical mastery with water magic in a way no other player has conceived. Its exact nature is unique to Eliza's build and knowledge.

Alchemy Ingredients & Herbs

Eliza cultivates a vast collection of rare herbs and alchemical ingredients using her plant growth acceleration magic. These range from common healing herbs to rare, deadly poisons. Her ingredient stockpile is the foundation of her combat effectiveness.

Sea's Edge (City)

Formerly known as Falcon's Hook, this city is renamed after Eliza becomes its regent. It serves as her seat of power and the anchor for her water mana well. She governs it on behalf of the Twilight Throne, managing its resources and defenses.

Potion Cache

An extensive collection of potions she has crafted over time. Includes healing potions, mana potions, poison antidotes, combat stimulants, and exotic concoctions with unusual effects. Her potions are considered superior to standard craftable items due to her alchemical expertise.

Notable Feats

Eliza's most impressive accomplishments

Created a Zone-Destroying Weapon

Using only alchemy and water magic, Eliza created a device capable of destroying an entire zone in AO. This feat rivals — and some argue surpasses — even Jason's most destructive abilities, all without necromancy or a lich transformation.

Earned the "Ice Witch" Title

Despite her real-life meekness, Eliza accumulated enough player kills to earn a feared reputation among the player base. The contrast between her gentle real-world personality and her in-game lethality is one of the most fascinating aspects of her character.

Stood Up to a God

Eliza finally confronted the Hippie and demanded respect. This act of assertiveness earned her the remote mana well bell — a reward from the Water God showing that even a capricious deity can respect someone who draws a line. This was a major character growth moment.

Governed Sea's Edge

As regent, Eliza manages a full city on behalf of the Twilight Throne. She handles administration, resource management, and defense — all responsibilities she never asked for but has grown into.

Pioneered Alchemical Combat

Eliza essentially invented a new combat style by combining alchemy with area-of-effect magic. The Obscuring Mist + potion infusion technique is uniquely hers and represents an innovative approach to AO's flexible magic system.

Nearly Destroyed Death and Taxes

One of the most infamous PvP guilds in AO was nearly wiped out by a single player — Eliza Zhao, the quiet alchemist who had never played a game before joining AO.
