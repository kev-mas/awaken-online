⚔ History & Origins

The evolution of Sea's Edge from a forgotten fishing village to the heart of the Water Affinity.

★ EARLY ERA

Falcon's Hook Founded

A small fishing village on the coast, serving as a quiet trading port for local fishermen and merchants. Named for the curved hook of the shoreline where peregrine falcons once nested.

⚔ BOOK 3.5 — APATHY

Lizardman Invasion

A horde of lizardmen emerged from the sea and laid siege to Falcon's Hook. The town's meager defenses were overwhelmed. Eliza Zhao and the members of Original Sin rushed to defend the settlement.

✦ THE TRANSFORMATION

Reborn as Sea's Edge

After repelling the invasion, Eliza leveraged her alchemy expertise and the favor of the Water God to transform the ruined village into a proper city. The settlement was renamed Sea's Edge — the first capital of the Water Affinity.

♛ THE CORONATION

Fluffy Takes the Throne

In an unprecedented move, the city was placed under the nominal rule of Fluffy — the Hippie's sheep companion. Eliza was appointed regent and governor, managing the city's affairs on behalf of the Twilight Throne and Original Sin.

🔥 BOOK 6 — ARMAGEDDON

The War of Affinities

Sea's Edge became a strategic stronghold during the conflict between affinities. Its water-mana reserves, alchemical stockpiles, and naval power made it a critical asset for the Twilight Throne's war effort.

⚔ City Defenses

Sea's Edge is protected by layered defenses that leverage the power of the Water Affinity.

⚓

Sea Wall

Twenty feet of enchanted stone curving along the coastline, reinforced with water-mana that causes it to regenerate from damage. Ballistae line the parapets.

⚔

Harbor Chain

A massive iron chain strung across the harbor entrance, raised and lowered by water-powered winches. Prevents enemy vessels from sailing into the port.

⚗

Water Mages

A cadre of water-attuned mages who patrol the walls and harbor. They can summon tidal waves, freezing mist, and water shields to repel attackers.

✈

Naval Militia

A fleet of swift warships crewed by veteran sailors and water-affinity fighters. They patrol the coastal waters and coral reefs, intercepting threats before they reach the city.

♞ Key Inhabitants

The people and beings who shape Sea's Edge.

♛

Fluffy

Ruler of Sea's Edge

A sheep. Formerly the Hippie's pet, now the unlikely sovereign of the Water Affinity's capital. Fluffy reigns from a custom throne in the Town Hall, tended to by dedicated NPCs.

⚗

Eliza Zhao

Regent & Governor

The de facto ruler of Sea's Edge. A master alchemist and member of Original Sin. Eliza rebuilt the city after the lizardman invasion. She manages trade, defense, and diplomacy on Fluffy's behalf.

☼

The Hippie

Water God

The Water Affinity's patron deity, a being of immense power. The Hippie has a strong presence in Sea's Edge and blessed the city's founding. His symbol is woven into the city's architecture and banners.

✦ Major Events

Key moments in the history of Sea's Edge.

⚓
★ PRE-GAME

Founding of Falcon's Hook

A small fishing settlement is established on the curved hook of the coastline. It grows slowly as a waypoint for coastal traders.

⚔
⚔ APATHY (BOOK 3.5)

Lizardman Invasion

A massive lizardman force attacks Falcon's Hook. Eliza Zhao and Original Sin arrive to defend the town. The battle reshapes the coastline and nearly destroys the settlement.

✦
✦ REBUILDING

Sea's Edge Founded

Eliza negotiates with the Twilight Throne and the Water God to rebuild and rechristen the settlement as Sea's Edge — the first Water Affinity capital.

♛
♛ PROCLAMATION

Fluffy Crowned Ruler

In a controversial and unprecedented decree, the Hippie installs Fluffy as the nominal ruler of Sea's Edge. Eliza is appointed regent. The announcement shocks the political landscape.

⚗
⚗ EXPANSION

Alchemy Quarter Established

Eliza's alchemy operations outgrow temporary workshops. A dedicated district is constructed, complete with laboratories, storage vaults, and a merchant exchange for potions.

♥
♥ MERFOLK ACCORD

Merfolk Quarter Opened

Sea's Edge becomes the starting city for Merfolk players. Submerged districts and tidal accommodations are built, establishing a permanent Merfolk population in the city.

🔥 ARMAGEDDON (BOOK 6)

The Affinity War

Sea's Edge serves as a strategic hub during the war. The city's alchemical stockpiles, naval fleet, and water-mage corps prove decisive in multiple engagements.

Sea's Edge continues to grow as a center of water affinity power, trade, and alchemical innovation. The city stands as proof of what a dedicated group of players can build.
