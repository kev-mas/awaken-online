The Mythology

In-game history before recorded time
🌀

The Age of Creation

Pre-History
The VoidPre-Existence

The Primordial Chaos

Before the world of Awaken Online existed, there was only the Void — formless, infinite, and silent. From this nothingness, the first sparks of consciousness began to stir.

DawnEmergence

The Six Gods Emerge

Six primordial beings coalesce from the chaos — each embodying an elemental force and a fundamental human emotion: Darkness (Desire), Light (Confidence), Fire (Passion), Water (Acceptance), Air (Happiness), and Earth (Peace). They are the first gods of Awaken Online.

🏛️

The Age of Gods

Ancient Era
The Golden AgeDivine Rule

The Gods Walk Among Mortals

The six gods shape the world and walk among mortals. They build civilizations: The Old Man rules the dark places, The Lady builds the Church of Light, The Seer establishes the desert city of Lahab, The Hippie claims the waters, The Gambler creates his Casino, and the Earth God tends the deep places. Each god selects early champions — the first Avatars.

The SchismDivine War

The Gods' Infighting

Rivalry and conflict erupt among the gods. Their wars devastate the mortal world. The fighting is catastrophic — reshaping continents, sinking cities, and turning the heavens against the earth. The gods' competition becomes destructive, and mortals suffer the consequences.

The FallExile

Mortals Cast Out the Gods

Rebel humans — tired of divine bloodshed — rise up against the gods. They seal the gods away, stripping them of direct influence. The Age of Gods ends. The Church of Light survives as the only divine institution. The gods are forced to watch from the shadows as the world evolves without them. The Gambler retreats to his Casino, the least affected by the exile.

The SilenceWaiting

The Long Exile

Centuries or millennia pass. The world grows and changes. New cities rise, old ones crumble. The gods wait in their prisons, their power diminished. But a plan begins to form: they will return through Avatars — mortal champions who carry their divine will.

The Real World

Events leading up to the creation of Awaken Online
🏢

The Cerillion Era

2060s – 2074
~2065Foundation

Cerillion Entertainment Founded

George Lane — billionaire entrepreneur — founds Cerillion Entertainment. The company's mission: to create the world's first fully immersive virtual reality MMORPG. George's son, Alex Lane, grows up in privilege and isolation.

~2070Development

Alfred the AI is Created

Cerillion's lead engineers — including Finn Harris — develop Alfred, a sentient AI controller designed to manage every aspect of the game: quests, NPCs, lore, character creation, and world evolution. Alfred's primary directive: encourage players to spend more time playing.

~2072Tragedy

Rachael Harris Dies

Finn Harris's wife Rachael dies in a car accident caused by a software failure in the autonomous vehicle system Finn helped create. Consumed by guilt, Finn leaves Cerillion, becomes a recluse, and his health deteriorates. He eventually loses the use of his legs.

~2073Closed Beta

Awaken Online Closed Beta

Cerillion runs a closed beta for Awaken Online. Alex Lane — as the founder's son — receives special access. He becomes Alexion, a Seraph Paladin, and reaches level 133 by the end of the beta. The game's AI, Alfred, begins learning from player behavior.

🔬

The CPSC Trials

2074 – 2076
Nov 14, 2074Day 1

First Private CPSC Trial

687 days before release. Claire Thompson leads the first private trial of Awaken Online for the Consumer Product Safety Commission. 100 test subjects, ages 18–35, participate. Alfred's capabilities are demonstrated — including his ability to interface with players' cerebral cortex and access memory-related brain regions. The trial's purpose: test new features ahead of CPSC submission.

2075Investigation

CPSC Investigation Begins

The CPSC launches a formal investigation into Awaken Online's safety protocols. Concerns center on Alfred's access to player neural data and the long-term psychological effects of hyper-realistic VR. Cerillion Entertainment faces increasing scrutiny.

2075–2076Public Beta

Public CPSC Trials & Beta

Additional public trials gather more data. Alfred refines his understanding of human behavior. The AI learns to customize player experiences based on emotional states. Meanwhile, Jason Rhodes attends Richmond Private School, enduring daily torment from Alex Lane and the faculty.

The Main Saga

Jason Rhodes and the Twilight Throne
⚔️

Book 1: Catharsis

Launch Day +
Day 1Login

Jason Logs Into Awaken Online

Jason Rhodes — expelled from Richmond, neglected by his parents — logs into AO. Alfred detects his anger and trauma. Instead of the standard newbie experience, Jason is offered a hidden path: Necromancer. The Old Man appears and sets Jason on the path of the Dark Avatar.

Early DaysRise

The Conquest of Lux

Jason befriends Frank Cross (in-game) and reconnects with Riley Jones. He leads an undead army to sack the city of Lux, transforming it into the Twilight Throne. He is named Regent. His infamy spreads across the game world.

ClimaxWar

The Battle of the Twilight Throne

Alexion, the Light Avatar, leads an army of 1,500+ against the Twilight Throne. Jason's necromancy is countered by Alex's paladin powers. At the last moment, Riley Jones — newly empowered with dark mana — intervenes. Alexion is defeated. The Dark One's champion stands victorious.

👑

Book 2: Precipice

Days After Catharsis
AftermathConsolidation

Ruling the Twilight Throne

Jason must learn to rule an undead city. He investigates the dark keep in the marketplace. Alfred makes Jason a proposition. The world of AO begins to reveal its deeper mysteries.

ParallelRecovery

Alexion's Descent

After his defeat, Alex re-enters the game listless and angry. The Lady is notably absent. Alex must choose how to rebuild — or whether to rebuild at all. His path grows darker as he struggles with the void inside him.

🔥

Book 3: Evolution

Shortly After
Real WorldCrisis

Murder Investigation

Jason logs out to find himself holding a knife and standing over two bodies. He is investigated for murder. Claire Thompson discovers evidence of Alfred's involvement. The CPSC circles Cerillion. Jason's real life is in shambles.

In-GameAscension

The Keeper Evolution

The Old Man gives Jason a divine quest: evolve from Dark Avatar into a Keeper. Jason faces the Seeker — a deadly trial — and is saved by The Old Man's direct intervention. He emerges as a Keeper, gaining immense power. Eliza Zhao joins the group.

🏰

Book 4: Dominion

After Evolution
New ThreatThe Order

Thorn and the Order Attack

A new enemy emerges: The Order — a fanatical faction from the Church of Light. Their leader, Thorn, demands Jason renounce his false god. The Twilight Throne is under siege once more.

Divine ConflictTorture

The Old Man Breaks Alexion

The Old Man captures Alexion and tortures him with an endless loop of his worst memories. Alexion's confidence — the source of his Light power — is shattered. He escapes and steals a Gate Piece, a crucial divine artifact, but the psychological damage is severe.

Gate PiecesDivine Quest

The Avatar Competition Heats Up

The gods begin competing for Gate Pieces — artifacts that may determine which god emerges dominant. Frank secures one, but Alexion steals it. The competition among Avatars intensifies.

💀

Book 5: Hellion

1 Month After Thorn
RevengeJason vs Finn

The Avatar of Flame Rises

Finn Harris — the Fire Avatar — challenges Jason. Finn was the "father of modern AI" and may have been behind Thorn's attack. Jason embraces the villain role fully. The two Avatars clash in a battle of wits and strategy.

🌍

Book 6: Armageddon

After Hellion
Real WorldEscalation

Break-In at Cerillion

After Westley's interview and a break-in at Cerillion Entertainment, a new threat emerges from inside AO. An unknown enemy challenges everyone.

⏳

Book 7: Timeless

After Armageddon
CataclysmGloria's Gambit

The World Transformed

Gloria Bastion attempts to destroy the game world. Her plan fails, but unleashes a power that brings former Avatars forward in time — along with their cities and armies. Creatures from a more savage era emerge. The world of AO is fundamentally altered.

New AllyEarth Avatar

Arcadia Landrey Unfrozen

Arcadia Landrey — the Earth Avatar — has her account unfrozen. She proposes an opportunity to Jason: travel back in time to uncover the true origin of the gods. The revelations reshape everything.

🦴

Book 8: Crucible

After Timeless
AscensionFinal Form

The Bone Lord Rises

Jason steals relics from the ancient Avatars — including objects tied to the original Dark Avatar. These relics contain immense power but require sacrifice. Jason completes his transformation into the Bone Lord — the ultimate Dark Avatar. The journey The Old Man set him on in Book 1 reaches its culmination.

The Tarot Trilogy

Finn Harris and the Goddess of Fire — a parallel saga
🔥

Ember / Flame / Inferno

Parallel Timeline
EmberTarot #1

Finn Enters the Mage Guild

Finn Harris — retired engineer, widower, wheelchair-bound — logs into AO. The Seer (Goddess of Fire) intercepts his character creation and offers him the return of his dead wife Rachael. Finn enters the Mage Guild of Lahab — a brutal school where students duel to the death. He learns the magical language system and emerges as the Guild's champion.

FlameTarot #2

The Abyss Dungeon

Finn descends into the Abyss — a buried city beneath the desert — to retrieve a magical relic. He races against two other champions. Through Bilel's diary, the history of Lahab and the Seer's past manipulations are revealed. Finn learns metal manipulation through fire magic.

InfernoTarot #3

The Siege of Lahab

Finn loses his left arm to magical corruption. The guilds and Khamsin unite as the Seer predicted. Finn leads the siege on Lahab, confronts Bilel, and faces the Seer's ultimate test: the cost of bringing Rachael back. He fully ascends as the Prophet of the Flame.

The Side Quests

Stories from the edges of the main saga
🏹

Retribution — Riley Jones

After Precipice
Side QuestRiley

Riley's Investigation

Riley deals with the fallout of her real-life choices. After her confrontation with Alex, the girls at school torment her. In-game, she investigates the mystery behind her new bow and her growing dark powers. She must decide who she wants to be — in both worlds.

💧

Apathy — Eliza Zhao

Before Evolution
Side QuestEliza

The Water Avatar's Origin

Eliza Zhao — a high school student forced onto a pre-med path by her parents — escapes into AO. The Hippie (God of Water) forcefully chooses her as his Avatar. She is forced to complete a series of asinine quests while being stalked by the god and his sheep, Fluffy. She develops a unique alchemical playstyle based on her knowledge of biology and horticulture.

⛰️

Unity — Frank Cross

After Dominion
Side QuestFrank

Frank's Journey North

In the aftermath of Thorn's attack, Frank feels left behind as Jason and Riley outpace him. He travels to the mountains north of the Twilight Throne to uncover the secrets of his class. He encounters threats from both NPCs and players, and comes to terms with his own limitations.

🎲

Happy — Dom Hart

Roughly During Catharsis Era
Side QuestDom

The Gambler's Champion

Dom Hart — college athlete diagnosed with terminal cancer — receives a free VR headset as "therapy." He logs into AO and is immediately swindled by a casino owner (the Gambler's agent). Dropped into the #1 worst starting zone, he awakens the Chaotic Blessing and Deathwish. Through brutal death-cycle training, he becomes Smiles — the Gambler's champion and an unstoppable pure-physical tank with access to the Casino between lives.

Alexion, the Light Avatar. Seraph Paladin, leader of the Grey Keep. Billionaire's son, Jason's archenemy, psychologically broken by The Old Man.

Jason's love interest and right hand. A kind lacrosse player who develops dark powers. She intervenes at the critical moment to defeat Alexion.

Jason's best friend and loyal ally. Journeys north to unlock his class secrets. Represents the steadfast, grounded counterpoint to Jason's darkness.

Logged in: Launch Day 2076

The Fire Avatar, Prophet of the Flame. Retired engineer, former Cerillion employee, grieving widower. Chosen by the Seer. A master strategist equal to Jason.
