Character Overview

Real Life — Frank Cross

  • School: Richmond Private School. Jason's only friend.
  • Appearance: Overweight, known for playing video games. Struggles with self-image and confidence.
  • Personality: Loyal, good-natured, but plagued by self-doubt. Often feels overshadowed by Jason and Riley. His journey in AO is as much about finding self-worth as it is about gaining power.
  • Relationship with Jason: His only real friend at Richmond. They stay in touch after Jason's expulsion. Their bond is the foundation of Frank's loyalty.
  • Struggles: Compares himself to others (Jason, Riley, Rebecca). Feels inadequate in both real life and the game. His journey in Unity forces him to confront these feelings.

In Game — Frank Cross

  • In-Game Name: Frank (same)
  • Class Path: Thaumaturge → Primal Shifter
  • Alignment: Lawful Evil
  • Affinities: All weak and equal — which gave him the unique Thaumaturge class, a jack-of-all-trades foundation. Later evolves into pure shapeshifting.
  • Role: Tank / Front-line fighter / Shapeshifter
  • Faction: Original Sin / Twilight Throne
  • Build: After his first death, dumped all stats into Strength to become the team's barbarian/tank.
  • Signature Moment: In Dominion, fought Alexion for a Gate Piece. Defeated and imprisoned him, but Alexion escaped and stole the piece. Later took his own solo journey north in Unity.

The Transformation

Frank's journey from Jason's gamer friend to a primal force of nature
Catharsis (Book 1)Real-Life Friend

The Only Friend

Frank is introduced as Jason's only friend at Richmond. While he doesn't appear in-game until Book 2, he stays loyal to Jason after the expulsion, keeping in touch with him. He's a typical gamer kid — overweight, good-natured, and deeply loyal. This foundation of trust makes him Jason's most reliable companion.

Precipice (Book 2)Thaumaturge → Barbarian

First Steps in AO

Frank joins Jason and Riley in the Twilight Throne. He reveals he had a rough start — all his affinities were equally weak, preventing him from becoming a mage. This gave him the unique Thaumaturge class. During a journey with Jason and Riley, Frank dies for the first time. After respawning, he makes a pivotal decision: dump all stats into Strength and become the team's tank. This is his first step toward finding his place in the group.

Dominion (Book 4)The Gate Piece Fight

Facing Alexion

With Jason busy consolidating power, Frank takes on a critical mission: securing a Gate Piece from Alexion. He fights the Light Avatar and defeats him, successfully imprisoning him. However, Alexion escapes in a controversial twist and steals the Gate Piece. Despite the outcome, Frank proves he can hold his own against one of AO's most powerful players.

Unity (Book 4.5)Primal Shifter Awakening

The Journey North

Feeling outpaced by Jason and Riley, Frank sets off on his own toward the snow-capped mountains north of the Twilight Throne to improve his shapeshifting abilities. He stumbles upon Haven — a hidden village of shapeshifters, druids, tamers, and growers. He meets Silver (a cold, skilled shifter), Alderas (a leader figure), and Abigail (a warm teacher). He encounters an ancient God, faces cultists, and for the first time, becomes a leader. He also falls in love. His class evolves into Primal Shifter, unlocking his true potential.

Hellion → Crucible (Books 5–8)The Faithful Lieutenant

Standing Beside the Dark Lord

Frank returns to the main storyline as Jason's left hand, fighting alongside him against Finn Harris, Death and Taxes, the ancient avatars, and the chaos of the lich transformation. He has fully embraced his Primal Shifter identity, able to shift between human, hybrid, and animal forms at will. In Armageddon, he witnesses Jason's apotheosis. In Crucible, he stands ready as Jason sacrifices his humanity.

Powers & Abilities

Frank's unique class and combat capabilities

Thaumaturge Foundation

Frank's unique class was born from having all affinities weak and equal. This gave him a jack-of-all-trades foundation that later specialized into shapeshifting. The Thaumaturge class allows him to adapt and learn abilities from multiple domains, making him incredibly versatile despite not excelling in any single affinity.

Versatile Adaptive

Shapeshifting — Primal Shifter

Frank's core ability. He can transform between human, hybrid (werewolf-like), and full animal forms. His primary animal form is a wolf. In hybrid form, he retains human dexterity with animal strength, claws, and fangs. Each form has advantages: human for speech/social, hybrid for combat, animal for speed/tracking. His shapeshifting is tied to his Primal Shifter class, unlocked during his journey in Unity.

Wolf Form Hybrid Form Full Beast

Tank / Barbarian Build

After his first death, Frank dumped all stats into Strength. He wears heavy armor and wields a two-handed weapon (an axe). He serves as the front-line tank for the Twilight Throne forces, absorbing damage and drawing aggro while Jason commands from the back and Riley picks off targets from range. His endurance is exceptional.

Damage Sponge High Strength Melee Combat

Nature Connection

Frank developed a deep connection to nature and primal forces through his journey in Haven. He learned from druids, shifters, tamers, and growers. He encountered an ancient God who tested him. This connection allows him to sense natural energies, track through wilderness, and tap into primal rage when fighting.

Gate Piece Combat

Character Progression

Frank's growth from sidekick to primal force

Build Philosophy

Frank is a Strength-based melee tank. After his first death, he went all-in on Strength, with secondary stats in Endurance and Constitution. His Thaumaturge foundation gives him unusual versatility, while his Primal Shifter evolution adds natural weaponry and mobility. Stat priority: Strength > Endurance > Constitution > Wisdom.

Character Arc

Frank's journey is about finding his own identity. He starts as "Jason's gamer friend" — always in the background, never the hero. His arc peaks in Unity where he becomes a leader for the first time, facing an ancient God, protecting a village, and falling in love. He emerges as a Primal Shifter — no longer just a sidekick, but a force in his own right.

Allies & Associates

Those who fight alongside the Primal Shifter

Jason Rhodes

Frank's best friend and the Dark Lord himself. They were friends at Richmond before AO. Frank is Jason's most loyal companion, following him through every transformation. Their bond is the longest-standing relationship in the series.

Riley Jones

Frank's teammate and fellow lieutenant of the Twilight Throne. They operate as Jason's right and left hands. Riley and Frank manage the city and fight alongside each other in every major battle.

Silver

A skilled shifter from Haven. Initially cold and dismissive toward Frank, she eventually becomes his teacher and love interest. She transforms into a wolf and has deep ties to the druidic community. Her real name is "Ana," revealed when children call her that.

Alderas

A leader figure in Haven. Initially skeptical of Frank, but willing to give him a chance. Represents the open-mindedness needed to overcome prejudice. Helps Frank find his place in the community.

Abigail

A bubbly teacher in Haven who leads the children's class. She welcomes Frank warmly and offers him a place to learn, despite his initial awkwardness with the children. A symbol of Haven's nurturing side.

Enemies & Rivals

Those who oppose the Primal Shifter

Alex Lane / Alexion

Frank fought Alexion in Dominion for a Gate Piece and defeated him in combat. However, Alexion escaped imprisonment and stole the piece. Their rivalry is personal — Alex represents everything wrong with privilege and cruelty, and Frank represents earned strength.

Haven's Cultists

During his journey in Unity, Frank encounters cultists who threaten Haven. These enemies are connected to the darker forces in the region. Frank must protect his new home from them.

Self-Doubt

Frank's greatest enemy is his own lack of confidence. He constantly compares himself to Jason, Riley, and others. His journey is as much about overcoming internal demons as external ones. Haven taught him to believe in his own worth.

Psychological Profile

Understanding Frank's inner world

The Loyal Friend

Frank is defined by his loyalty. He stuck with Jason when he was expelled, followed him into a game where he struggled, and never wavered even as Jason became a terrifying lich lord. His loyalty is his greatest strength and occasionally his weakness — he sometimes defines himself by who he follows rather than who he is.

The Comparison Trap

Frank constantly compares himself unfavorably to Jason, Riley, and even Rebecca (a figure from Unity). This self-doubt holds him back from stepping into leadership. His journey in Unity forces him to confront this and realize he has his own unique strengths.

The Primal Self

Frank's shapeshifting is a metaphor for his inner transformation. The wolf represents instincts, freedom, and power that he never accessed as an overweight gamer kid. Learning to shift is learning to accept all parts of himself — including the primal, powerful ones. His hybrid form is the balance between his human self-doubt and his animal confidence.

Finding Leadership

For the first time in Unity, Frank becomes a leader. He protects Haven, faces an ancient God, and makes decisions that affect others. This is a turning point. He returns to the main storyline not as a sidekick but as an equal. The experience reshapes his self-image permanently.

Love & Belonging

Frank falls in love in Haven, finding a connection beyond the game. This relationship grounds him and gives him something to fight for beyond Jason's cause. It represents his growth into a full person with his own desires and attachments — no longer just "Jason's friend."
