Character Overview

Real Life

  • Full Name: Arcadia "Cady" Landrey
  • Age: Adult (established professional, older than Jason Rhodes)
  • Occupation: High-powered management consultant with an MBA
  • Personality: Controlled, calculating, cynical, secretive, master manipulator in business. Suppresses emotion professionally. Driven by guilt and a desire to fix what's broken.
  • Background: A shark in the boardroom. Master saleswoman who knows how to read a room and manipulate executives. Her professor taught her: "Don't make sales, make customers — making a sale wasn't about selling a product. It was about telling a story."
  • Family Tragedy: Her younger sister Tara was in a catastrophic car accident that left her a "hollow, still-breathing husk" on life support. Cady was estranged from her family but rushed to the hospital when her mother called. Doctors told her Tara could remain on life support indefinitely.
  • Recruitment: While at the hospital, attorney Francis Rosencrantz approached her with a mysterious job opportunity — the hook that led her into Cerillion Entertainment's orbit and, ultimately, into Awaken Online.

In Game

  • In-Game Name: Arcadia (or Cady)
  • Class Path: Earth Avatar — Time Magic specialist
  • Affinity: Earth — Time / Memory / Foundation
  • Alignment: Lawful Neutral
  • Status: Ancient Avatar — account frozen in the game's past, then unfrozen by Gloria Bastion's cataclysm
  • God: Avatar of the Earth God (name unknown)
  • Titles: The Earth Avatar, The Timeless One, Cady
  • Unique Mechanic: Her powers are tied to Time rather than traditional mana. She can manipulate time loops, temporal acceleration, and even take people back to the game's past.
  • Starting Era: One of the original avatars from AO's early days — before the main series timeline.

The Transformation

Cady's journey from a boardroom shark to a time-bending ancient avatar
Pre-Series (Feb 2076)Consultant → Recruit

The Pitch

Cady is a high-powered management consultant pitching to Sinclair Logistics. She's a master of reading rooms and manipulating executives. Her sister Tara's catastrophic car accident shatters her world. At the hospital, attorney Francis Rosencrantz approaches her with a mysterious job offer — the first thread pulling her toward Cerillion Entertainment and Awaken Online.

Pre-Main SeriesRecruit → Earth Avatar

Becoming the Earth Avatar

Cady enters Awaken Online and is chosen by the Earth God to become an Avatar. She plays during the game's early era — before Jason, before the Twilight Throne, before any of the events of the main series. She establishes herself, her city, and her power base in the savage, fresh game world. Her account is eventually frozen, preserved in the game's code as one of the "ancient avatars."

Timeless (Book 7)Frozen → Unfrozen

The Cataclysm — Unfrozen

Gloria Bastion's attempt to destroy the game world has catastrophic consequences: it brings the original avatars forward in time, along with their cities and armies. Cady's frozen account is unfrozen. She emerges into a world centuries (in-game) advanced from her own era. Jason and his allies are besieged, with nowhere to go. Cady is uniquely positioned to help — but on her own terms.

Timeless (Book 7)Stranger → Reluctant Ally

Teaming Up with Jason

Cady approaches Jason with a proposal: use her time-based abilities to travel back to the game's savage past, before the cataclysm. The early dynamic is tense — Cady is secretive and refuses to answer Jason's questions, leading to friction. But as the journey progresses, they bond. Reviewers note powerful chemistry between them (the author intended a big-sister dynamic). Together, they uncover the origin of the in-game gods and the true history of AO.

Crucible (Book 8)Ally → Key Asset

The Relics & The Heist

Jason steals relics from the ancient avatars — likely including objects tied to Cady's era. She continues as a key ally. The ancient avatars and their armies remain a threat, but Cady's knowledge of the past proves invaluable. The book culminates in a massive heist and a cliffhanger ending that sets up the series' final confrontation.

Powers & Abilities

A comprehensive catalog of Cady's time-bending arsenal

Time Magic (Mana Replacement)

As the Earth Avatar, Cady's powers are tied to Time rather than traditional mana. This unique mechanic replaces the conventional magic system with temporal manipulation. She can accelerate, decelerate, loop, and rewind time within localized areas. The full extent of her temporal powers includes time loops, time travel, temporal acceleration, and localized time stasis.

Time Mana Time Loops Temporal Acceleration

Temporal Travel (The Past)

Cady's signature ability: she can take herself and others back in time to the game's past — to the era when the original avatars were active. In Timeless (Book 7), she takes Jason back to the savage, fresh game world before the main series events. This allows them to uncover the origin of the in-game gods and seek solutions to the present-day cataclysm.

Time Travel Past Forward Origin Discovery

Earth Affinity Magic

As the Earth Avatar, Cady commands the element of Earth — associated with Foundation, Memory, and Time. Her earth magic is typically broad in scope, reflecting the stable, patient nature of the element. She can manipulate stone, soil, and terrain, creating barriers, reshaping landscapes, and drawing on the "memory" of the earth itself.

Terrain Manipulation Stone Barriers Earth Memory

Ancient Knowledge

As one of the original avatars from AO's early days, Cady possesses knowledge of the game's true history that no modern player has access to. She knows the origin of the in-game gods, the ancient conflicts, and the secrets buried in the game's code. This knowledge is a weapon more powerful than any spell — and she guards it carefully.

Origin Knowledge God Lore Ancient Secrets

Strategic Mind

Before becoming an Avatar, Cady was a high-powered management consultant and master negotiator. She reads people, manipulates situations, and plans multiple moves ahead. Her strategic approach complements and sometimes clashes with Jason's tactical cunning. She is patient, methodical, and always has an angle.

Negotiation Strategic Planning People Reading

Frozen Account Resilience

Cady's account was frozen in the game's code for what felt like ages. When Gloria Bastion's cataclysm unfroze her, she retained all her original power but had to adapt to the modern game world. This unique status means she exists slightly "out of sync" with normal reality — a side effect that may contribute to her temporal abilities.

Out of Sync Preserved Power Cataclysm Survivor

Known Spells & Skills

Catalogued temporal and earth abilities

Temporal Rift

Signature

Opens a rift in time that allows Cady and her allies to travel to the game's past. The primary ability used in Timeless. Requires immense concentration and a stable anchor point in history.

Time Loop

Tier 3

Encases a localized area in a time loop, causing events to repeat until the loop is broken. Useful for training, reconnaissance, and psychological warfare.

Temporal Acceleration

Tier 2

Speeds up Cady's personal time stream, allowing her to move and react faster than normal perception. Similar to Finn's Haste but derived from time magic rather than perception enhancement.

Earth Spire

Tier 1

Raises a spire of stone from the ground. Basic earth magic. Can be used for offense, defense, or terrain shaping.

Stone Barrier

Tier 1

Creates a wall of compacted stone. Durable and reliable. Can be maintained indefinitely as long as Cady feeds it temporal energy.

Memory of the Earth

Unique

Draws on the earth's memory to reveal past events that occurred in a location. Allows Cady to see historical events as if they were happening before her. Used for uncovering ancient secrets.

Time Stasis

Tier 3

Freezes a target or area in time temporarily. Victims are fully aware but unable to move. The duration depends on the target's resistance and Cady's power level.

Temporal Anchor

Tier 2

Creates a fixed point in time that Cady can return to later. Allows her to "save" a location and time coordinate for future temporal travel.

Earthquake

Tier 3

Shakes the ground in a wide radius, toppling enemies and structures. Combines earth manipulation with temporal vibration for enhanced effect.

Paradox Ward

Unique

A defensive temporal field that protects Cady from the negative effects of time manipulation — paradox backlash, temporal fragmentation, and timeline corruption. Essential for a time mage's safety.

Character Progression

Stat analysis and growth trajectory

Build Philosophy

Cady is a time mage and earth specialist — a unique hybrid that replaces the traditional mana system with temporal energy. She invests in Intelligence (temporal precision, spell complexity), Wisdom (temporal energy regeneration), and Charisma (negotiation, manipulation). Her earth magic provides durability while her time magic provides utility and battlefield control.

Power Trajectory

Unlike most characters who grow linearly, Cady's power was frozen at its peak and then unfrozen into a world that had advanced past her. She didn't grow weaker during her stasis — rather, the world grew different. Her journey in Timeless involves learning to apply her ancient abilities to modern problems while uncovering secrets that even she didn't know.

Allies & Associates

Those who cross paths with the Earth Avatar

Jason Rhodes

The Dark Lord and Cady's primary partner in Timeless. Their dynamic is tense then cooperative. Reviewers note powerful chemistry. Author intended a big-sister dynamic. They uncover the origin of the gods together.

Tara Landrey

Cady's younger sister. Catastrophic car accident left her brain-dead on life support. This tragedy is Cady's core motivation and emotional wound. Everything she does is, in some way, for Tara.

Francis Rosencrantz

The attorney who recruited Cady at the hospital. A recurring figure in the series who connects people to Cerillion Entertainment. He was the catalyst for Cady entering the AO world.

Bran Sinclair

CEO of Sinclair Logistics. Cady was pitching to him in the prologue (Feb 2076). Represents her professional life before everything changed.

Gloria Bastion

The antagonist whose actions unfroze Cady. Gloria's attempt to destroy the game world caused the cataclysm that brought the ancient avatars forward in time. Cady's emergence is directly tied to Gloria's actions.

Earth God

The deity who chose Cady as an Avatar. Patron of the Earth Affinity. His name is not yet revealed in available sources. His domain includes Foundation, Memory, and Time.

The Ancient Avatars

Cady's place among the original powers of Awaken Online

The Original Avatars

Cady is one of several "ancient avatars" — players who were chosen by the gods in AO's early days, before the main series timeline. These avatars existed in a savage, fresh game world where the rules were still being written. Their accounts were frozen in the game's code, preserved as historical artifacts. Gloria Bastion's cataclysm brought them all forward in time along with their cities and armies, creating chaos across the modern game world.

The Cataclysm

Gloria Bastion's attempt to destroy the game world had an unintended side effect: it unfroze the ancient avatars, crashing their entire era into the present. This created a temporal and geographical disaster — ancient cities appearing where modern ones stood, armies from different centuries colliding. Cady emerged from this chaos with knowledge and power no modern player could match.

Origin of the Gods

Through Cady's time travel, she and Jason discover the true origin of the in-game gods. These revelations reshape everything the characters thought they knew about AO. The Earth Avatar's journey is as much about uncovering history as it is about changing the future.

George Lane's True Nature

The epilogue of Timeless reveals the true character of the owner of Cerillion Entertainment — George Lane (Alex's father). His actions are described as "despicable." Cady's ancient knowledge likely plays a role in exposing the deeper conspiracy behind the game's creation.

Notable Feats

Cady's most impressive accomplishments

Mastered Time Magic

Cady wields a completely unique magic system — time manipulation that replaces traditional mana. She is the only known character in AO with this ability, making her one of the most unique avatars in the series.

Took Jason Back in Time

Cady used her Temporal Rift to transport Jason Rhodes — the most powerful avatar of the modern era — to the game's savage past. This feat of time magic is unprecedented in AO's history.

Uncovered the Origin of the Gods

Through her journey with Jason, Cady helped uncover the true origin of the in-game gods — a revelation that reshapes the entire understanding of AO's world.

Survived Temporal Stasis

Being frozen in the game's code for centuries and then unfrozen by a dimensional cataclysm would destroy most minds. Cady emerged intact, retaining her power and her wits.

Exposed George Lane

The revelations in Timeless's epilogue — exposing the true, despicable nature of Cerillion Entertainment's owner — are directly tied to Cady's ancient knowledge and time travel.

Negotiated with Jason Rhodes

Cady successfully convinced the Dark Lord himself to trust her and follow her plan — no small feat given Jason's paranoid nature. Her consulting skills translated perfectly into avatar diplomacy.
