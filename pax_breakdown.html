♻The History of Pax

From the dawn of AO's early era through the present — a city that refused to die.

★ Early Game Era

Founding of Pax

Arcadia Landrey — known as Cady — one of the early avatars, discovers the Earth affinity and establishes Pax as its capital. The city is grown from living stone and earth, a sanctuary of resilience in the untamed wilds of AO's first generation.

⚑ Rise of Pax

The Golden Age

Pax flourishes. The Verdant Spire rises at the city's heart. The Stone Market becomes a hub of earth-forged goods. Terraced farms line the outer slopes. Cady's power grows as the Earth God's chosen — a force to be reckoned with before the age of Cerillion's Twilight Throne.

⚠ The Freezing

Account Frozen — Preserved in Code

Cady's account is locked and frozen by circumstances beyond her control. Everything — her avatar, her city, her army, her entire legacy — is preserved as static data within AO's codebase. Pax becomes a ghost city, unreachable, sleeping in the depths of the game's memory.

⚠ The Lost Years

Forgotten by the World

While Jason, Cerillion, and the Twilight Throne rise to power — while the Heretic and the Dark Finance reshape AO — Pax remains frozen. The city becomes legend, a rumor passed between early players. The age of the ancient avatars fades into myth.

🔥 Timeless (Book 7)

The Unfreezing

When Cady is finally unfrozen in the Timeless expansion, her entire city and army are transported from the ancient past to the present day. Pax emerges from static code into living reality — a wildcard no faction anticipated. The old world crashes into the new.

♥ Present Day

Uneasy Alliance

Cady becomes an uneasy ally of Jason. Pax now stands as a third force — not aligned with the Twilight Throne, the Heretic's shadow, or any other power. The city represents the "old world" of AO, built before Cerillion's rules rewrote the game.

⛩City Layout & Districts

Pax is not built — it is grown. Living stone and root form a city that breathes.

♛

Verdant Spire

The seat of Arcadia Landrey and the heart of Pax. This colossal spire of living stone and crystalline earth rises at the city's center, wrapped in vines and glowing with ambient earth-mana. Cady's personal chambers and the war council reside here.

⚑

The Stone Market

The trade and commerce hub where earth-forged goods are exchanged. Though Pax is largely self-sufficient, the Stone Market remains the social heart of the city — where stonecraft, alchemical reagents, and rare minerals change hands.

⚗

The Root Warrens

Underground residential and crafting district. A maze of root-lined tunnels and earthen chambers where the city's craftsmen, alchemists, and artisans live and work. Lit by bioluminescent moss and earth-crystals.

☼

The Terraces

Terraced farms carved into the slopes surrounding Pax. Magically enriched soil and controlled irrigation from underground aquifers allow year-round cultivation. The city never wants for food.

⛰

The Earthworks

The defensive fortifications of Pax — living walls of regenerating stone, raised battlements, and earth-elemental watchposts. The Earthworks encircle the entire city and are the first line of defense against any invader.

☠

The Deep Halls

An ancient repository carved into the bedrock beneath Pax. Contains records, artifacts, and knowledge from the earliest days of AO — much of it predating the Twilight Throne. The Deep Halls are a library of forgotten history.

☘

Grove of Remembrance

A sacred grove at the edge of the city where massive stone trees stand as monuments to those lost in Pax's history. Each tree is carved with names and stories. The Earth God's presence is said to be strongest here.

⚔

East Garrison

The military training grounds and barracks. Earth-elemental guardians are shaped and awakened here. The garrison trains both human NPC defenders and coordinates the elemental sentinels that patrol the city walls.

⚔Defenses & Military

Pax was designed to endure. Its defenses are as old as the city itself — grown from the earth, not built.

⛰

Living Stone Walls

The walls of Pax are not static — they are alive. Grown from enchanted stone seeded with earth-mana, they regenerate damage over time, sealing breaches and reforming under assault. A siege against Pax is a siege against the mountain itself.

☣

Earth-Elemental Guardians

Awakened constructs of stone and crystal, bound to defend the city. Ranging from small sentinels to colossal golems the size of buildings, they are tireless, fearless, and respond directly to Cady's will through the Earth affinity.

♻

Root Networks

Beneath the surface, an immense network of enchanted roots lies dormant — until invaders arrive. These roots can entangle entire battalions, pull enemies underground, or create barriers in seconds. The very ground beneath an invader's feet is a weapon.

⚐

Underground Chokepoints

Every approach to Pax above or below ground is a kill zone. Tunnel networks collapse on command. Caverns flood with earth-mana, petrifying anything inside. The interior of Pax is a labyrinth designed to confound and destroy organized military formations.

"They did not build walls. They grew them. And what the earth grows, it does not easily surrender."

♞Key Inhabitants

The people — and the presence — that give Pax its soul.

♕

Arcadia Landrey

♛ The Earth Queen — "Cady"

The founder and ruler of Pax. An ancient avatar frozen in AO's code for years, unfrozen in Timeless. Cady is a morally grey character — neither hero nor villain, driven by survival and the protection of her people. Her loyalty is to Pax first, above all factions.

☮

The Earth God

∞ The Unnamed — Balance & Endurance

The genderless, nameless deity of the Earth affinity — associated with balance, endurance, and the natural cycle of growth and decay. The Earth God's power flows through Pax, granting the city its regenerating walls, fertile terraces, and living stone. A presence more than a person.

⚓

Ancient NPCs of Pax

⚑ The First Generation

The NPC citizens of Pax are among the oldest in AO — scripted and leveled in the game's early era. They remember a world before the Twilight Throne. Their knowledge of forgotten lore, lost crafting techniques, and ancient magic is unmatched in the present day.

★

The Elemental Elders

⚗ Greater Awakened Constructs

The oldest and most powerful earth elementals bound to Pax. Each embodies a facet of the city — the Wall-Elder, the Root-Elder, the Deep-Elder. They possess limited sentience and have guarded Pax since its founding, older than any mortal inhabitant.

⚙Economy & Resources

Pax is entirely self-sufficient — its economy is one of growth and refinement, not trade.

⛰

Stonecraft

Master artisans shape living stone into weapons, armor, tools, and architecture. Items from Pax are infused with earth-mana, granting durability and regenerative properties.

⚗

Earth-Mana Forging

A unique crafting discipline that combines raw earth-mana with rare minerals to create items that grow stronger with the user. Exclusive to Pax's master forgers.

✦

Mineral Alchemy

The Deep Quarries yield rare crystalized mana ores, obsidian, and mineral reagents. Cady's alchemists brew potions and elixirs with effects undiscovered by modern AO crafters.

☼

Terraced Farming

Magically enriched soil and ancient irrigation techniques allow year-round cultivation. Pax produces more food than it consumes, with reserves that could last a decade.

🔥Major Events

The key moments that shaped Pax across two eras of AO history.

✦
ERA 1 — THE FOUNDING

The Groundbreaking

Cady channels the Earth God's power for the first time, raising the Verdant Spire from raw stone. The founding of Pax marks the establishment of the Earth affinity's capital and the first major Earth-aligned city in AO.

⚑
ERA 1 — THE GOLDEN AGE

The Conclave of Stone

The first gathering of earth-aligned players and NPCs. Cady formalizes the city's government, establishes the Deep Halls as a repository, and awakens the first Elemental Elders. Pax becomes a true capital.

⚠
ERA 1 — THE FREEZING

The Sundering of Cady

Cady's account is frozen due to circumstances beyond her control. The entire city — every NPC, every structure, every elemental — is locked in a static state within AO's code. Pax vanishes from the living world.

⚠
ERA 2 — THE DARK AGE

The Forgotten City

Through the rise of the Twilight Throne, the Heretic, and the great wars of AO — Pax is nothing but a rumor. Old players speak of a city of living stone, but no one can find it. The city sleeps in code.

🔥
ERA 2 — TIMELESS (BOOK 7)

The Return

The Timeless expansion shatters the barriers keeping Cady's account frozen. When she logs in, her entire city and army come with her — transported from the ancient past to the present day. The world of AO reels as an entire civilization materializes.

♥
ERA 2 — PRESENT DAY

The Uneasy Pact

Cady forms a tentative alliance with Jason and the Twilight Throne. Neither fully trusts the other, but both recognize the value of friendship in a world of enemies. Pax becomes a wildcard in the balance of power — answerable to no one but itself.
