Character Overview

Real Life

  • Age: 18 at series start
  • Appearance: Longish brown hair, skinny frame, vivid crystalline-grey eyes that appear black in certain light. Later becomes more toned due to Alfred's physical simulation during gameplay.
  • Status: Loner, outcast, neglected by parents. Expelled from Richmond Private School after a setup by Alex Lane.
  • Personality: Sharp intellect, pragmatic, morally ambiguous. Finds purpose and power in AO after feeling powerless in real life.

In Game

  • In-Game Name: Jason (same)
  • Class Path: Necromancer → Dark Avatar → Seeker → Keeper → Lich (Bone Lord)
  • Race: Human (later undead/lich)
  • Affinity: Dark — Desire
  • Alignment: Chaotic Evil
  • Faction: Original Sin / Twilight Throne
  • God: Avatar of the God of Darkness
  • Titles: The Dark Lord, Bone Lord, The Dark One, Regent of the Twilight Throne, Welfare (derogatory, given by Alex)

The Transformation

Jason's harrowing journey from a bullied teenager to an undead lich lord who sacrificed his own body for power
Catharsis (Book 1)Human → Necromancer

The Awakening

After being expelled from school, Jason logs into AO for the first time. The AI (Alfred) detects his anger and trauma, granting him access to a hidden class: Necromancer. He meets Morgan, Rex, and Jerry in Lux, and his first kill triggers his eyes to turn entirely black — foreshadowing his dark path.

Catharsis (Book 1)Necromancer → Dark Avatar

Fall of Lux — Rise of the Twilight Throne

Jason orchestrates the sacking of Lux, transforming it into the Twilight Throne, a dark undead city. He is named Regent. He defeats Alexion's army through cunning tactics, establishing himself as the Avatar of the God of Darkness.

Evolution (Book 3) / Dominion (Book 4)Dark Avatar → Seeker → Keeper

The Keeper Evolution

Through trials and battles, Jason evolves into a Seeker and then a Keeper. Rampant growth of power. His physical form begins to change — horns grow from his forehead, his hair and eyes blacken, his skin turns pale white, and tattoos of dark mana appear across his body.

Armageddon (Book 6)Keeper → Lich (Bone Lord)

Ascension into Undeath — The Surgery

The most dramatic transformation. Jason replaces his limbs with bone and mana-infused constructs. He then performs a self-surgery — removing his own vital organs and transplanting his Najima (soul/core essence) into a new body made entirely of bone. All while Alexion live-streams it to the entire world.

Crucible (Book 8)Lich — The Ultimate Sacrifice

The Crucible

Jason has "already sacrificed his body." To unlock the stolen relics, he must give up his humanity itself. He reforges himself into something stronger — fully embracing the Bone Lord identity. His form is now a walking skeleton infused with dark mana, animated by the Najima in his chest.

Powers & Abilities

A comprehensive catalog of Jason's arsenal across all forms

Undead Minions

The core of Jason's power. He can raise and control undead minions — zombies, skeletons, bone constructs, and bone demons. Minion cap is tied to Willpower (1 per 10 Willpower). He specializes in swarming tactics, overwhelming enemies with quantity and strategic placement.

Zombies Skeletons Bone Demon Bone Constructs

Necromancy & Dark Magic

Jason wields dark mana to cast curses, debuffs, and control spells. He chose debuff curses and additional minion types over direct attack spells, making him a force multiplier rather than a direct damage dealer. His power is tied to his Dark affinity — Desire.

Curses Debuffs Dark Mana Willpower Scaling

Lich Physiology

After transforming into a lich, Jason gained immense durability. His bone limbs are reinforced constructs that move unnaturally smooth. His vital organs are gone — his Najima (soul core) is now his life force, residing in his chest cavity. He can survive damage that would destroy a normal body. Joints click and scrape like bone.

Bone Limbs Najima Core Damage Resistant

Dark Aura & Presence

Jason radiates an aura of cold, palpable darkness. The air chills around him. Shadows cling to him. The ground he walks on frosts over. This aura commands undead and terrifies living beings. The intensity has grown with each transformation.

Chill Aura Shadow Cling Undead Command

Stealth & Deception

Jason is a master of cunning strategy. He uses his hood to conceal his face, misdirection to confuse enemies, and real-time intelligence from player-uploaded footage to track enemy movements. He won the war against Alexion by exploiting information his enemies posted online.

Tactical Genius Information Warfare

Kingdom Management

As Regent of the Twilight Throne, Jason rules an undead city. He manages resources, expands territory, secures villages, grows military strength, and handles diplomacy. He "farms" players to grow his undead population. His leadership is pragmatic and ruthless.

City Management War Tactics Resource Expansion

Character Progression

Stat analysis and growth trajectory across the series

Build Philosophy

Jason dumps the majority of his stat points into Willpower, which increases his mana pool and minion cap (1 minion per 10 Willpower). He receives 5 stat points per level. This makes him a glass cannon commander — fragile personally but devastating through his army.

Minion Growth

At level 1: 1 minion (average). By level ~20: ~50 minions. By later books (sacrificing his body for the lich form): potentially hundreds of undead under his command at once, sustained by his Najima's mana generation.

Enemies & Rivals

The forces arrayed against the Dark Lord

Alex Lane / Alexion Arch-Nemesis

Race: Seraph | Class: Paladin / Light Avatar. The billionaire son of George Lane (owner of Cerillion Entertainment). Alex is Jason's archenemy in both real life and in AO. He is the Avatar of the Goddess of Light. In his transformed state, Alexion has massive feathered wings, a flaming sword, and shining plate armor — the perfect angelic counter to Jason's undead darkness. He lives-streamed Jason's lich surgery to the world.

Finn Harris Rival Avatar

Avatar of Flame. The "father of modern AI." A brilliant engineer with a tragic past. His fire affinity opposes Jason's darkness. They clash over gate pieces and territory. Finn is a master strategist on par with Jason, making their conflicts particularly brutal.

Thorn

A mysterious and deadly enemy who attacked the Twilight Throne. Defeated Jason and his companions in their first encounter. Forced Jason to evolve and consolidate power.

Death and Taxes

An infamous PvP guild hired by Jason's unknown enemies. They become a recurring threat, eventually nearly wiped out by Eliza Zhao's water magic in Armageddon.

Gloria Bastion

Attempted to destroy the game world. Her actions unleashed the ancient avatars forward in time, creating chaos across AO. A terrorist-like figure within the game's narrative.

PewPew

A personal enemy of Jason's who has a vendetta against him. Forced to work together in Armageddon during the avatar trials but remains hostile.

Key Artifacts & Items

Powerful objects in Jason's possession

Relics of the Ancients

Stolen from the ancient avatars who returned in Timeless. These relics contain immense power but require sacrifice to unlock. Jason's crucible involves using these against his enemies.

Gate Pieces

Mysterious fragments tied to the gods of AO. Jason and the other avatars fight over these. They seem to grant access to divine-level power or knowledge.

Najima

Jason's soul-core. The essence of his being. Transferred into his bone body during the Armageddon surgery. Now pulses in his chest cavity as his lich-phylactery. Destroying it would mean true death.

Magical Hood

A hood that shrouds his face in impenetrable darkness. Acquired mid-series. Allows him to conceal his monstrous visage from allies and enemies alike.

Bone Body

His current form — a body constructed entirely of bone, reinforced with dark mana. Articulated limbs, exposed ribcage, curved horns on the skull. The ultimate expression of his lich transformation.
