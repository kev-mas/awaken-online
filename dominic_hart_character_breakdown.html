Character Overview

Real Life

  • Full Name: Dominic "Dom" Hart
  • Age: College age
  • Status: College athlete (football) on athletic scholarship
  • Diagnosis: Terminal cancer — given approximately 6-12 months to live
  • Financial Situation: Cannot afford prohibitively expensive treatment
  • Personality: ISTP, Enneagram 7w8. Brutal, pragmatic, desperate. Treats NPCs as living beings. Approaches the game with a unique perspective — he has nothing left to lose.
  • Entry into AO: A nurse at the clinic enrolls him in a clinical trial using the Awaken Online VR headset as "therapy." His father repaired slot machines, so Dom grew up around casinos — an experience that proves strangely relevant to his in-game starting zone.
  • Initial mindset: Overwhelmed with despair, he approaches the game as a way to pass his final days. He has no expectation of surviving — in real life or the game.

In Game

  • In-Game Name: Smiles / Smiley
  • Class Path: Physical Tank — unconventional luck-based build
  • Affinity: Air — Happiness / Chaos
  • Alignment: Chaotic Neutral
  • Faction: Death or Taxes (Founder & Leader)
  • God: The Gambler — God of Air / Happiness (not a true Avatar, but a specially empowered champion)
  • Titles: Smiles, The Gambler's Champion, The Man Who Cannot Die, Leader of Death or Taxes
  • Starting Zone: Aislen — a jungle sky-island city voted the "#1 worst starting location in Awaken Online." Run by the Jackals, a mafia-like guild that controls new players. Even the plant life can kill you.
  • Build: Pure physical tank — no mana, no spells. Pours everything into Vitality, Strength, Dexterity, and Endurance.

The Diagnosis

Dominic Hart is a college football player on an athletic scholarship when he's diagnosed with terminal cancer. Given 6-12 months to live. He cannot afford the prohibitively expensive treatment. A nurse at the clinic enrolls him in a clinical trial using the Awaken Online VR headset as "therapy." He has nothing left to lose.

Happy (Book 5.5)Noob → Unique Build

Into the Worst Starting Zone

Dom spawns in Aislen — a jungle sky-island city voted the "#1 worst starting location in Awaken Online." Located way south of the Twilight Throne, it's controlled by the Jackals, a mafia-like guild that preys on new players. Even the plant life is lethal. Most players are stuck here, unable to escape. Dom, having nothing to lose, refuses to be trapped.

Happy (Book 5.5)The Gambler's Blessing

Chaotic Blessing — Luck Over Mana

Dom catches the attention of The Gambler (God of Air / Happiness), who breaks tradition by empowering Dom as his champion rather than a true Avatar. The Gambler replaces Dom's mana pool with a luck modifier, introducing a luck inversion mechanic where good and bad fortune constantly swap places. Since Dom has no mana, he cannot cast spells — forcing him into a pure physical tank build. This confusing blessing becomes one of the most unique mechanics in the series.

Happy (Book 5.5)Deathwish — Grinding Through Death

Dying to Get Stronger

Dom discovers the Deathwish ability: it drastically reduces his respawn time, allowing him to accelerate growth through intentional suicide loops. He dies repeatedly on purpose — using death as a training tool for extreme physical conditioning, resistance training, and stat grinding. This masochistic playstyle is a refreshing contrast to Jason and Finn, who rarely die. Each death makes him stronger.

Happy (Book 5.5)The Casino — Purgatory

Access to The Casino

Instead of reliving his death in helpless stasis like other players, Dom awakens in The Casino — a purgatory dimension run by The Gambler. Here, he is fully conscious, able to move, observe, and reflect between deaths. Eventually, he grants his allies access to The Casino too, giving Death or Taxes a massive strategic advantage. This realm becomes Dom's sanctuary and command center.

Happy (Book 5.5)Founding Death or Taxes

Building a Family of Outcasts

Dom builds Death or Taxes ("Death and Taxes") — a guild of outcasts who all hold grudges against the mainline protagonists. Each member was broken by Jason, Finn, or their allies. Dom gives them purpose, a place to belong, and a leader who refuses to stay dead. His guild is positioned as a future rival to Jason's Original Sin — a dark mirror of the Twilight Throne's inner circle.

Chaotic Blessing (Passive)

The Gambler replaces Dom's mana pool with a luck modifier. This introduces a luck inversion mechanic — good and bad fortune constantly swap places unpredictably. Since Dom has no mana, he cannot cast spells, forcing him into a pure physical build. This confusing blessing is one of the most unique mechanics in Awaken Online — a constant gamble that makes every moment unpredictable.

Luck Modifier Inversion Mechanic No Mana Pool

Deathwish (Active)

Drastically reduces Dom's respawn time. Allows him to accelerate growth through intentional suicide loops — dying repeatedly on purpose. He uses this for extreme physical conditioning, resistance training, and stat grinding. Each death makes him tougher. This masochistic playstyle is unique among the series' protagonists — Jason and Finn avoid death; Dom embraces it.

Fast Respawn Death Grinding Stat Acceleration

Access to The Casino (Purgatory Realm)

Instead of reliving his death in helpless stasis, Dom awakens in The Casino — a purgatory dimension run by The Gambler. Here he is fully conscious, able to move, observe, and reflect between deaths. He can grant allies access too, giving Death or Taxes a massive strategic advantage. The Casino serves as Dom's sanctuary, planning room, and command center.

Purgatory Dimension Ally Access Strategic Hub

Unstoppable Tank Build

Dom pours every stat point into Vitality, Strength, Dexterity, and Endurance. He is a pure physical powerhouse — no magic, no ranged attacks, no subtlety. Just overwhelming physical force backed by the ability to die and come back stronger. His tank build combined with Deathwish makes him an almost unstoppable force that simply refuses to stay down.

High Vitality Strength Focus Endurance Grind

Wingman (Battle Chicken)

Dom's unique companion — a Battle Chicken named Wingman. Wingman drains 10% of Dom's stats in exchange for an ever-growing pet that eats everything. This bizarre trade-off reflects Dom's luck-based playstyle: sacrificing a chunk of his power for a chaotic, unpredictable ally. Wingman grows in proportion to Dom's own growth.

Character Progression

Stat analysis and growth trajectory

Build Philosophy

Dom is a pure physical tank — the anti-mage. With no mana pool and no spellcasting ability, he invests everything into Vitality (HP, damage resistance), Strength (melee damage), Endurance (stamina, physical resilience), and Dexterity (movement, reaction speed). His Deathwish ability means every death is an investment — each respawn makes him statistically stronger than before.

Growth Through Death

Dom's power curve is unlike any other character. While Jason relies on minions and Finn on magical mastery, Dom grows through sheer repetition of dying. His "training segment" in Happy takes up the first ~35% of the book, showing his transformation from a dying noob to an almost unstoppable force. His ability to grant allies access to The Casino makes his entire guild harder to kill.

Key Artifacts & Items

Powerful objects and resources in Dom's possession

The Casino Key

Dom's metaphorical and literal access to The Casino purgatory realm. Allows him and his allies to respawn into a conscious planning space rather than helpless stasis. The heart of Death or Taxes' strategic advantage.

Slot-Marked Gear

Dom's armor and equipment are adorned with slot machine and casino motifs — a reflection of his connection to The Gambler. These items may have hidden luck-based properties that activate unpredictably.

Wingman's Feeding Stockpile

An ever-growing supply of materials, items, and enemies that Wingman the Battle Chicken has consumed. The chicken's ability to eat almost anything makes it a mobile disposal and growth engine.

Death-Grind Resistance

Not a physical item, but an accumulated trait: Dom's body has been hardened through thousands of deaths. Each respawn builds resistance, making him progressively harder to kill. This "item" is earned through sheer suffering.

Notable Feats

Dom's most impressive accomplishments

Survived the Worst Starting Zone

Aislen is voted the "#1 worst starting location in Awaken Online" — controlled by mafia guilds, lethal wildlife, and designed to trap new players. Dom not only survived but escaped and thrived.

Mastered Death as a Tool

While every other player fears death, Dom weaponized it. His Deathwish ability lets him accelerate growth through intentional suicide loops — an approach no other protagonist uses.

Built Death or Taxes From Scratch

Dom built a guild of broken, discarded players who all hold grudges against Jason, Finn, and their allies. He gave them purpose, belonging, and a leader who refuses to die. The guild is positioned as a future rival to Original Sin.

Gained Access to The Casino

Instead of suffering in death stasis, Dom awakens in The Casino — a conscious purgatory dimension. He can grant this access to allies, giving his entire guild a massive strategic advantage over every other faction.

Defeated and Converted Booker

Dom defeated a former antagonist guild leader named Booker (Smokes) and converted him into an ally. This shows Dom's ability to turn enemies into loyal guild members through respect and strength.

Refused to Break

Given a terminal diagnosis, no money, and a game that starts him in literal hell, Dom refused to give up. His relentless, grinning defiance in the face of certain death is his greatest feat.
