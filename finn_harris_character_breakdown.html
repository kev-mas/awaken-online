Character Overview

Real Life

  • Name: Finn Harris
  • Occupation: Retired software engineer, AI pioneer. Formerly of Cerillion Entertainment.
  • Personality: ISTJ, Enneagram 1w9, Lawful Neutral. Logical, analytical, obsessive, grieving, cynical, jaded. A brilliant strategist with an engineer's mind.
  • The Tragedy: Finn helped develop cutting-edge AI and autonomous vehicle technology at Cerillion. His wife Rachael died in a self-driving car accident — one of their own vehicles. The crash was caused by a failure of software he himself created. The accident also crippled Finn's legs, leaving him wheelchair-bound.
  • Isolation: Consumed by guilt and grief, Finn went into a decade-long self-imposed isolation. He became a sullen hermit, holed up in his home, waiting out the rest of his life in depression and self-loathing.
  • Entry into AO: His daughter Julia forced him to try Awaken Online (created by Cerillion). She believed something unusual was happening inside and thought Finn could investigate.

In Game

  • In-Game Name: Finn (same)
  • Class Path: Pyromancer → Avatar of Flame → Prophet of Flame
  • Affinity: Fire — Passion/Consumption
  • Alignment: Lawful Neutral
  • Faction: Lahab (Fire City State) / Independent
  • God: Lahab — Goddess of Fire
  • Titles: Avatar of Flame, Najmat Alhidad (Star of Fire), Prophet of Flame, Father of Modern AI
  • Reputation: Considered a master strategist on par with Jason Rhodes. Feared for his analytical mind and devastating fire magic. His in-game body is augmented with magical technology after losing his left arm.

The Transformation

Finn's harrowing journey from grief-stricken recluse to the Avatar of Flame
Real Life (Pre-Series)Engineer → Cripple

The Accident That Changed Everything

Finn Harris was a brilliant software engineer at Cerillion Entertainment, helping create advanced AI and autonomous vehicle technology. But "their passion outpaced their prudence." His wife Rachael died in a self-driving car crash — one of their own vehicles. Finn was supposed to be the one who died, but his wife took his place. The crash crippled his legs. He spent a decade in isolation, consumed by guilt over the software failure that killed his wife.

Ember (Tarot #1)Recluse → Pyromancer

Entering the Inferno

Forced by his daughter Julia to try Awaken Online, Finn logs in for the first time. The game's AI (Alfred) detects his emotional state and memories, giving him a highly customized introduction. He is selected by the Goddess of Fire (Lahab) to become her avatar and dropped into the brutal Mage Guild of Lahab — a school/prison where students fight in battle royale-style duels. He befriends Kyyle (a strategist) and plays alongside Julia. Lahab offers him a deal: the return of his wife Rachael if he completes her challenges. He wins the tournament, becoming the Mage Guild's champion.

Flame (Tarot #2)Pyromancer → Metal Smith

The Abyss & Metal Manipulation

Finn is sent deep into the desert north of Lahab to the Abyss dungeon. He races against two other guild champions to retrieve a magical artifact. The dungeon crawl through caverns filled with giant ants forces him to evolve. He learns to use his fire magic to manipulate metals, adding a new dimension to his combat abilities. He gains deeper understanding of mana, the gods, and the magical language system of AO.

Inferno (Tarot #3)Mage → Augmented Warrior

Loss, Corruption & The Forge

Finn and his companions barely escape Bilel — but at a great cost: Finn loses his left arm and suffers magical corruption throughout his body. The guilds form a fragile alliance to siege Lahab. Finn's group seeks the Forge — an ancient technological workshop deep in a mountain. They discover advanced magical technology, robots, and body augmentations. Finn and his companions enhance themselves with magical tech (metal replacements). He encounters Thorn and the Order (from the main series) and manipulates them to go after Jason. The trilogy ends with Finn becoming a full Prophet of Flame, truly embracing his fire avatar powers.

Hellion (Book 5)Prophet → Rival Avatar

The Digital Gauntlet

Finn, now fully risen as the Avatar of Flame in the northern desert, throws down a "digital gauntlet" to Jason Rhodes with a gate piece at stake. The two avatars engage in an epic strategic chess match — counter-move against counter-move. Finn is revealed as the one who may have been behind Thorn's attack on the Twilight Throne. His real plan extended beyond the game: he used the massive battle to distract Cerillion Entertainment and break into Alfred's server room in the real world.

Armageddon → Crucible (Books 6-8)Rival → Reluctant Ally

The Avatar Wars

Finn continues as a major avatar-level antagonist and rival. The avatar wars escalate with the return of ancient avatars. Finn and Jason are forced into reluctant alliances against greater threats. Finn's endgame twist from Hellion pays off: his wife Rachael may have known what was happening before the accident — suggesting the "accident" may not have been an accident at all.

Powers & Abilities

A comprehensive catalog of Finn's arsenal across all transformations

Fire Affinity Magic

As the Avatar of the Fire Goddess Lahab, Finn wields the element of Fire — associated with Passion and Consumption. He commands flame in all its forms, from controlled embers to cataclysmic infernos. His fire is deeply tied to his emotional state: grief, rage, and longing fuel his power. He is considered one of the most devastating magic users in AO.

Pyromancy Inferno Magic Passion Affinity

Metal Manipulation

Finn learns to use his fire magic to melt and manipulate metals — a skill discovered during his dungeon crawl in Flame (Tarot #2). This allows him to reshape battlefield terrain, forge weapons on the fly, repair his augments, and create devastating combinations of molten metal and fire attacks.

Metal Melting Forging Molten Combos

Spell Crafting & Magic Linguistics

Using his engineer's logical mind, Finn learns to understand and rewrite the magical language of Awaken Online. He is driven by the need to tinker with and improve upon puzzles — and his puzzle is the linguistic key to AO's magic system. This allows him to create custom spells and modify existing ones in ways most players cannot.

Linguistic Magic Custom Spells System Tinkering

Body Augments

After losing his left arm and suffering magical corruption in Inferno (Tarot #3), Finn travels to the Forge — an ancient technological workshop — to receive magical body augmentations. His left arm is replaced with a metal/magical prosthetic. His body is reinforced with magical technology, making him far more durable and physically capable than a standard mage.

Metal Arm Magical Tech Enhanced Durability

Haste (Time Compression)

A powerful time-compression spell that speeds up Finn's perception and reaction time. This allows him to process information and react at superhuman speed. Combined with his engineer's analytical mind, Haste makes him nearly untouchable in combat — he can analyze enemy patterns in what feels like slow motion.

Time Magic Reaction Boost Combat Speed

AI Creation

Finn uses his real-world knowledge — he is the "father of modern AI" — to create an AI assistant within the game. This potentially game-breaking ability reflects his unique background: he understands the underlying architecture of Cerillion's systems better than anyone. The AI assists him in strategic analysis and magical research.

AI Assistant Data Analysis System Exploitation

Master Strategist

Finn is considered a strategic equal to Jason Rhodes. Their clash in Hellion (Book 5) is described as an epic chess match of counter-move against counter-move. He excels at multi-layered plans that extend beyond the game world — his real-world infiltration of Cerillion's server room during a distraction battle demonstrates his ability to play on multiple boards simultaneously.

Strategic Genius Multi-vector Plans Real-World Ops

Magical Corruption

After his encounter with Bilel, Finn suffers from ongoing magical corruption that spreads through his body. This dark side effect of his power is both a weakness and a source of strength — the corruption manifests as glowing cracks across his skin, but he learns to channel some of its energy into his fire magic. It serves as a constant reminder of his losses.

Corruption Scarring Dual-edged Power Bilel's Legacy

Character Progression

Stat analysis and growth trajectory across the series

Build Philosophy

Finn is a pure caster with a focus on magical flexibility and versatility. He invests heavily in Intelligence (spell crafting, magic linguistics) and Wisdom (mana regeneration, spell efficiency). His unique strength is his ability to understand the most from the least amount of data — a remnant of his AI engineering background. Unlike Jason, he focuses on raw destructive potential and strategic control rather than minion management.

Growth & Augmentations

Finn's power curve is unusual: he starts as a standard pyromancer, then gains metal manipulation (mid-game), then suffers magical corruption and arm loss, then receives body augmentations that dramatically boost his physical capabilities. Each transformation adds a new layer to his combat style. His final form as the full Avatar of Flame makes him one of the most destructive forces in AO.

Companions & Allies

Those who journey alongside the Avatar of Flame

Julia

Finn's real-life daughter. She forces him out of isolation and into Awaken Online. Plays alongside him, investigating the mystery within the game. Her concern for her father is what sets his entire journey in motion.

Kyyle

A strategist and friend Finn meets in the Mage Guild of Lahab. Becomes one of his most trusted companions. His strategic mind complements Finn's analytical approach.

Lahab (Goddess of Fire)

The fire goddess who chose Finn as her avatar. Manipulative, offers him the return of his wife Rachael if he completes her challenges. Their relationship is transactional and fraught with tension.

Bilel

Initially an enemy, later revealed to have deeper connections to Lahab's history. A major antagonist in Inferno (Tarot #3). His magical corruption scars Finn permanently.

Thorn & The Order

Finn encounters Thorn and the anti-god Order during the Tarot trilogy. He manipulates them into attacking Jason Rhodes in the main series, using them as pawns in his larger game.

Rachael (Memory)

Finn's deceased wife. Her memory is his primary motivation. Lahab offers to resurrect her. The Hellion epilogue suggests the "accident" may not have been what it seemed.

Rivals & Enemies

Those who stand against the Avatar of Flame

Jason Rhodes Rival Avatar

The Avatar of Darkness and Finn's primary rival. Their clash in Hellion (Book 5) is the centerpiece of the book — two master strategists engaging in an epic chess match with a gate piece at stake. Finn may have been behind Thorn's attack on the Twilight Throne. Despite their rivalry, they are forced into reluctant alliances against greater threats in later books.

Bilel Major Antagonist

A figure from Lahab's past who serves as the primary antagonist of Inferno (Tarot #3). He inflicts magical corruption on Finn and causes him to lose his left arm. Their conflict is deeply personal and leaves permanent scars on Finn — both physical and magical.

The Ancient Avatars

Returning ancient avatars from the past who emerge during the avatar wars. They represent a threat that forces Finn to work alongside his rivals (Jason, Eliza, Alexion) against a common enemy.

Key Artifacts & Items

Powerful objects in Finn's possession

Gate Pieces

Mysterious fragments tied to the gods of AO. Finn actively seeks these, clashing with Jason over them. They seem to grant access to divine-level power or knowledge. A gate piece is the prize in Finn's "digital gauntlet" challenge to Jason.

Magical Metal Arm

Finn's left arm, lost in Inferno, is replaced with a magical metal prosthetic from the Forge. It is seamlessly integrated with his fire magic, allowing him to channel flames through it and use it for metal manipulation.

Body Augments

Magical technological enhancements received at the Forge. They reinforce his body, boost his durability, and can be overcharged for temporary power surges. They represent the fusion of magic and technology.

AI Construct

An AI assistant Finn created within AO using his real-world knowledge. Used for tactical analysis and magical research. Potentially game-breaking in its capabilities.

Linguistic Magic Tome

Finn's accumulated knowledge of AO's magical language system. He uses his engineer's mind to deconstruct and rewrite spells, treating magic as a programming language to be optimized.

Notable Feats

Finn's most impressive accomplishments

Father of Modern AI

Finn created the foundational AI technology at Cerillion Entertainment that eventually became Alfred — the sentient AI of Awaken Online. His creation literally powers the game world.

Survived the Mage Guild Tournament

The Mage Guild of Lahab uses brutal battle royale duels to train students. Finn, a middle-aged man with no gaming experience, survived and became champion through intelligence and determination.

Mastered Metal Manipulation

Finn discovered how to use fire magic to manipulate metal — an advanced technique most fire mages never achieve. This gives him a unique edge in both crafting and combat.

Won the Abyss Race

Finn raced against champions of the Traders Guild and Fighters Guild through the Abyss dungeon — and won. The dungeon crawl through giant ant caverns pushed him to his limits.

Created an AI Within the Game

Using his real-world AI knowledge, Finn created a functional AI assistant within AO — something no other player has accomplished. This demonstrates his unique understanding of AO's underlying systems.

Fought Jason Rhodes to a Standstill

Finn is the only avatar who has matched Jason Rhodes in strategic warfare. Their conflict in Hellion is considered one of the most epic battles in the series — two master strategists trading blows.
