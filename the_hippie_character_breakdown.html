Divine Profile

Identity

  • Name: The Hippie (no proper name is ever revealed)
  • Titles: God of Water, Lord of Acceptance, Patron of Contrariness
  • Affinity: Water — associated with the emotion of Acceptance
  • Alignment: Chaotic Neutral
  • MBTI: ENFP | Enneagram: 7w8 (The Enthusiast)
  • Core Drive: Fear of being deprived or trapped in pain. Core desire to be free and experience joy.
  • Appearance: Dresses like a stereotypical hippie with a laid-back aesthetic. Often wears a floppy hat. Carefree, relaxed posture that belies his mischievous nature.

Personality

  • Contrariness seems to be his very affinity. He does the opposite of what you'd expect, every single time.

The Water Affinity

Understanding the power of Water and Acceptance

Philosophy of Water

In Awaken Online, the Water affinity is associated with Acceptance — the ability to flow around obstacles, adapt to circumstances, and embrace reality as it is. Water does not fight the riverbank; it shapes itself to the container. This philosophy is central to The Hippie's character: he forces Eliza to accept herself, her situation, and her power. He is annoyance as a teaching tool.

Water Magic in AO

Water magic under The Hippie's domain is versatile and adaptive. It encompasses healing, alchemy, defensive magic, and water-based offensive spells. The Hippie's Avatars and followers excel at alchemy and herbalism — Eliza creates the most potent potions in the game. The water capital of Sea's Edge provides a passive bonus to water-based abilities for any water-affiliated players or NPCs within its range of influence.

The Acceptance Paradox

The Hippie embodies a beautiful paradox: he is the god of Acceptance, yet he refuses to accept anything as it is. His contrariness — doing the opposite of what's expected — is itself a form of teaching acceptance. By forcing Eliza into situations she didn't want, he taught her to accept challenges, accept her own strength, and accept that life doesn't go according to plan.

Contrariness as Affinity

The official NPC description in Armageddon (Book 6) states: "Contrariness seems to be his affinity." This suggests that The Hippie's divine nature is fundamentally oppositional. Where other gods embody a single trait, The Hippie embodies the rejection of expectation itself. He is the god who does the unexpected — which makes him the most unpredictable and therefore most dangerous god in the pantheon.

The Lore

The history of the Water God and his place in the pantheon

The Age of Gods

The Hippie was one of the six primordial gods who held a prominent place in the early days of Awaken Online. Alongside The Old Man (Dark), The Lady (Light), Lahab (Fire), The Gambler (Air), and the Earth God, he walked among mortals and shaped the world. His domain was Water — the element of Acceptance, flow, and adaptability.

The Fall

After the gods' infighting devastated the world, rebel humans rose up and cast them out. The Hippie was banished along with the rest of the pantheon. But even in exile, his contrariness likely made him the most difficult god to contain — how do you imprison a being whose very nature is to do the unexpected?

The Return — The Avatar Competition

In the modern era, the gods have returned but can no longer act directly. They must work through Avatars. The Hippie chose Eliza Zhao — a burned-out, apathetic high school senior — as his Avatar. On the surface, this seems like a terrible choice. But it's perfectly in character: he chose the one person who didn't want to be chosen, forced her into the role, and used his annoying methods to forge her into the Ice Witch — one of the most feared players in AO.

Sea's Edge — The Water Capital

The town of Falcon's Hook is conquered by Eliza and Original Sin and renamed Sea's Edge. It becomes the Water Affinity's capital city. The Hippie's influence flows through it. In a move that is either a joke or a stroke of genius, Fluffy (the black sheep) is appointed as its ruler, delegated by Eliza. The city serves as the starting location for Merfolk players and a hub for water-affiliated activities.

The Stalking — Forced Avatarhood

The Hippie stalks Eliza from the moment she logs into AO for the first time. She wanted to tend a garden and escape her stressful life. Instead, she is ambushed by an annoying hippie and a black sheep who force her to become a water mage and his Avatar — entirely against her will. This is the most The Hippie entrance imaginable: instead of a grand ceremony, it's an annoyance ambush.

Apathy (Book 3.5)Divine Questing

The Asinine Quests

The Hippie sends Eliza on a series of seemingly pointless, bizarre quests around Falcon's Hook. Each quest appears to waste her time — but each one secretly forces her to grow. She learns alchemy, herbalism, water magic, and combat. She gains confidence. She builds relationships. The Hippie's "useless" quests are actually a carefully designed curriculum for turning a passive, apathetic girl into a powerful Avatar.

The Reward — Standing Up to a God

After Eliza finally stands up to The Hippie and demands respect, he rewards her with a bell that grants her remote access to the water mana well in Sea's Edge. This is a dramatic power boost — she can now draw on her city's mana reserves from anywhere in AO. The Hippie, despite his annoyance, recognizes and rewards assertiveness. This is the closest he comes to admitting his methods had a purpose.

Jason and Frank travel to Falcon's Hook and encounter The Hippie on his island with thousands of lizardmen. The avatars of Fire (Finn), Water (Eliza), Dark (Jason), and Light (Alex) meet at Barrow to team up against The Gambler (Air). Eliza represents The Hippie, making her entrance as the Water Avatar. The Hippie himself is listed as an NPC, described simply as having contrariness as his affinity. The event is an olympics-style competition against the guild Death & Taxes.

The full extent of the Water God's abilities

Water Mana Mastery

The Hippie is the source of all Water mana in Awaken Online. He controls the essence of the Water affinity — the power of Acceptance, flow, and adaptability. Every water mage, alchemist, and healer draws their power from his domain.

Water Mana Flow Magic Acceptance Power

Avatar Empowerment

The Hippie grants his Avatar — Eliza Zhao — access to the Water Mana Well in Sea's Edge. After she earns his respect, he gives her a bell for remote access, allowing her to draw on the city's reserves from anywhere. He can design quests that evolve her abilities and unlock new powers — even if those quests seem pointless.

Contrariness — Divine Chaos

The Hippie's unique power: he does the opposite of what is expected. This isn't just personality — it's a divine ability. He can subvert predictions, break patterns, and introduce chaos into any situation. In a world where gods and players constantly scheme, The Hippie is the ultimate wildcard. His contrariness is explicitly called out as his affinity in the official game lore.

Chaos Affinity Pattern Breaking Divine Wildcard

Avatar Selection (Against Will)

The Hippie can choose an Avatar without their consent — a power unique among the gods. Eliza never wanted to be a water mage. The Hippie forced the role upon her anyway. This suggests his divine authority overrides player agency, making him one of the most intrusive gods in the pantheon.

Eliza creates the most powerful potions in the game and a weapon capable of destroying entire zones — the "Farm" WMD. This alchemical blessing is a unique expression of the Water affinity's creative, adaptive nature.

Character Progression

The Hippie's influence and power across the series

Power Scaling

As a primordial god, The Hippie's baseline power is absolute within his domain. Unlike other gods who rely on their Avatar's strength to act, The Hippie's contrariness makes him effective regardless of his Avatar's power level — he can always do the unexpected. However, his ability to project influence across the world scales with Eliza's growth and the development of Sea's Edge.

Influence Trajectory

The Hippie's influence grows as Eliza transforms from an apathetic student into the Ice Witch. His capital of Sea's Edge becomes a major power center. His bizarre methods are vindicated by Eliza's success. The fan theory that Fluffy is the true power adds an intriguing layer — the question of who is really in control remains deliberately ambiguous.

Relationship with Eliza

The heart of the Water Affinity storyline

Stage 1: Forced Bond

The Hippie stalks Eliza from her first login and forces her to become his Avatar. She is furious, confused, and completely not interested. He doesn't care. Their relationship begins with zero consent on her part — the most antagonistic god-Avatar dynamic in the series.

Stage 2: Annoyance as Education

The Hippie sends Eliza on a series of asinine quests. She hates him. She openly says she hates him. But the quests are secretly building her into a powerhouse. She learns alchemy, water magic, combat, and confidence — all while complaining about how annoying her god is.

Stage 3: Standing Up

Eliza finally confronts The Hippie and demands respect. Instead of punishing her, he rewards her with the remote mana well bell. This is the turning point — he wanted her to grow a spine. He was testing her, and she passed.

Stage 4: Adversarial Respect

By the later books, Eliza and The Hippie have an established adversarial-comedic relationship. She still calls him annoying. He still wastes her time. But there's mutual respect beneath the bickering. She became the Ice Witch because of him, and he chose her because he knew she could.

Trivia & Fan Theories

The deeper mysteries of the Water God

Fluffy is the Real God

A popular fan theory suggests that Fluffy the black sheep is the actual Deity of Water, and The Hippie is merely a front or figurehead. This parallels Alfred's relationship with Onyx, the black cat. Fluffy is always watching, always silent, and appointed ruler of Sea's Edge — while The Hippie acts as the distracting, annoying public face.

No Proper Name

The Hippie is the only god in the pantheon whose true name is completely unknown. Every other god has at least a title or epithet. The Hippie only has a nickname. This may be intentional — his contrariness extends to his very identity.

The Bell's Significance

The remote mana well bell that The Hippie gives Eliza is more than just a power boost. It represents control — she can now draw on Sea's Edge without his permission. It may also be a tracking device, a test, or a connection that transcends the game's normal mechanics.
