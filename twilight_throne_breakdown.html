THE TWILIGHT THRONE

Seat of the Bone Lord — Capital of the Undead Kingdom

Once the city of Lux, reborn in darkness. A necropolis of bone and shadow ruled by Jason Rhodes, the Dark Avatar of the God of Darkness. From these black walls, a kingdom was forged and an empire rose.

Origin & Rise

From Lux to Necropolis

"He had taken a city and turned it into a monument to death. The Twilight Throne. A fitting name for a kingdom of the damned." — Awaken Online: Catharsis
Book 1 — Catharsis

The Sack of Lux

Jason leads his undead army against the city of Lux, a major NPC-controlled trade hub. He orchestrates a masterful siege, overrunning the city with skeletons and dark magic. The once-bustling city of light falls in hours. Jason is named Regent of the newly-christened Twilight Throne by the game system, transforming it into the capital of the undead faction.

Foundation
Book 1 — Catharsis

The Battle of the Twilight Throne

Alexion, the Light Avatar, leads an army of 1,500+ players and NPCs against the fledgling city. Wielding a flaming sword and riding a flying Seraph mount, he dominates the battlefield. Jason's necromancy is hard-countered. At the last moment, Riley Jones — newly-empowered with dark mana — intervenes and decapitates Alexion. The siege is broken. The Twilight Throne survives its first great trial.

First Defense
Book 2 — Precipice

The Dark Keep

Jason investigates the Dark Keep that looms over the city's marketplace. This act leads to a chain of events that will determine the city's survival. He must grapple with the challenge of ruling an undead city where the population cannot procreate. His solution: "farming" enemy players through an arena system, converting their deaths into new undead citizens.

Governance
Book 3 — Evolution

Consolidation

With real-world pressure mounting (Jason is investigated for murder), the Twilight Throne faces in-game threats from Alexion's revived forces and the Light-aligned church. Jason must grow the city's power while his real life crumbles. The Twilight Throne becomes his only refuge, and he pours everything into strengthening it.

Growth
Book 4 — Dominion

The Order Attacks

Following Jason's evolution into a Keeper, a new enemy emerges: The Order, a fanatical faction from the Church of Light led by Thorn. They demand Jason renounce his false god. The Order defeats Jason and Original Sin in their first encounter. Jason must consolidate power: secure villages, establish income streams, and grow military strength. Meanwhile, something stirs the native undead around the city.

Crisis
Book 5 — Hellion

The Flame Avatar's Challenge

Finn Harris, the Avatar of Flame, emerges in the northern desert and throws down a gauntlet to Jason — with a gate piece at stake. Their clash is an epic strategic chess match. Frank Cross travels to the snowy mountains north of the Twilight Throne, discovering Haven and unlocking his Primal Shifter potential. The Twilight Throne's influence expands northward.

Expansion
Book 6+ — Armageddon & Beyond

The Bone Lord's Seat

Through the chaos of ancient avatars' return, the Crucible, and Jason's final transformation into the Bone Lord, the Twilight Throne endures. New towns are established, the guild Original Sin grows, manufacturing operations flourish, and the city evolves from a desperate refuge into the true dark capital of the game world. Eliza governs Sea's Edge as a satellite. The city has survived avatar wars, gods, AI rebellions, and the relentless march of enemies.

Outer Defenses

Thick black walls surround the city, patrolled by undead sentries. Defensive enchantments, traps, and magical wards protect against siege. The main gate is a massive bone-and-iron construct that can seal the city entirely.

Defenses & Military

The Unliving Army of the Dark Lord

☠

Undead Horde

The primary military force. Skeletons, zombies, bone constructs, death knights, and specialized undead units. Population sustained through the arena system and battlefield conversions.

⚔

Rex — Commander

Loyal NPC warrior and veteran of Lux's guard. Became one of Jason's earliest allies and commands the Twilight Throne's armed forces with tactical discipline and unwavering loyalty.

★

Original Sin Guild

Jason's player guild. A collection of dark-affinity players who help govern the city and fight alongside the undead army. They provide strategic depth, class diversity, and real-player coordination.

▲

City Walls & Wards

Fortified black walls reinforced with dark magic. Magical wards detect intruders, repel siege weapons, and inhibit healing magic of enemy forces. The main gate is enchanted to withstand battering rams.

⚙

Necromantic Reinforcement

Jason's necromancy allows him to raise fallen enemies mid-battle, turning casualties into reinforcements. This makes sieges against the Twilight Throne exceptionally costly — every death strengthens the defenders.

✦

Frank Cross — Tank

The Thaumaturge-turned-Primal Shifter serves as the city's premier front-line defender. His strength-based build, heavy armor, and shapeshifting abilities make him nearly unstoppable in direct combat.

The Regent / Bone Lord

The Dark Avatar and absolute ruler of the Twilight Throne. His word is law. He commands the undead, directs the guild, and shapes the city's destiny.

Left Hand / Primal Shifter

Jason's best friend and the city's champion. Commands the front lines. Governs in Jason's absence. His journey north expanded the Twilight Throne's influence.

Jason's anchor and closest confidante. The one who saved the city by decapitating Alexion. Manages day-to-day operations and serves as the city's moral compass.

Regent of Sea's Edge

Water mage and ally. Governs Falcon's Hook (renamed Sea's Edge) on behalf of the Twilight Throne. Her administrative skills and alchemy are invaluable to the kingdom.

Military Commander (NPC)

Veteran Lux guard. Loyal to Jason from the earliest days. Commands the undead army with discipline. One of the few NPCs Jason fully trusts.

Master thief and former innkeeper. Runs the Twilight Throne's underground economy. His network of informants and smugglers extends across the continent.
