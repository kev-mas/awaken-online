Character Overview

Real Life — Alex Lane

  • Age: 18 at series start (same class as Jason)
  • Appearance: Blonde hair, striking blue eyes, athletic physique, stunningly good-looking. "Angelic expression" that warps into a cruel smirk.
  • Family: Son of George Lane, billionaire owner of Cerillion Entertainment (creator of Awaken Online). Family on the board of several Fortune 500 companies.
  • Personality: Sociopathic tendencies from childhood — began killing small animals "to see what happens." Parents paid off authorities and threw money at the problem. Feels a constant void-like emptiness, fills it with cruelty and video game power.
  • School: Richmond Private School. Star quarterback. Popular and wealthy. Bullies Jason relentlessly, orchestrates his expulsion, derisively nicknames him "Welfare."

In Game — Alexion

  • In-Game Name: Alexion
  • Class Path: Paladin → Light Avatar (Avatar of the Goddess of Light)
  • Race: Seraph (winged angelic race)
  • Affinity: Light — Confidence (not morality; pure narcissistic certainty)
  • Alignment: Chaotic Evil (despite angelic appearance)
  • Faction: Grey Keep (opposing faction to Jason's Twilight Throne)
  • God: Avatar of the Goddess of Light
  • Personality in AO: Cruel, arrogant, believes himself invulnerable. Post-loss to Jason, becomes listless, hollow, and desperate for redemption/vengeance.

The Transformation

Alexion's journey from golden-boy bully to the Seraph of Light
Catharsis (Book 1)Human → Paladin

The Rise of Alexion

Alex enters Awaken Online and, true to his competitive nature, quickly rises as a powerful Paladin. He establishes himself as a leader of the Grey Keep, the premier Light-aligned city. His fame grows as he leads armies against dark-aligned players. He becomes the Avatar of the Goddess of Light, chosen for his supreme Confidence — the core trait of the Light affinity.

Catharsis (Book 1)Paladin → Light Avatar

The War for Lux

When Jason sacks Lux and transforms it into the Twilight Throne, Alexion responds with righteous fury. He mobilizes a massive army of players and NPCs. He posts videos and screenshots of his march, believing himself invulnerable. Jason exploits his arrogance — launching harassment attacks, exploiting his lack of sentries, and ultimately defeating him in a climactic battle. Riley's intervention saves Jason, but Alexion is humiliated and hollowed by the loss.

Precipice / Evolution (Books 2–3)The Hollow Period

Listless & Angry

After his defeat, Alex re-enters the game listless and angry. His reputation is in tatters. He drowns his emptiness in alcohol at real-world parties, assaults partygoers who mock him. In AO, he plots revenge against Jason but struggles to find his footing. His motivations are described as "childish" — simply angry he isn't the best, without deeper development.

Dominion (Book 4)The Gate Piece

Outmaneuvered — But Escapes

Alexion fights Frank for a Gate Piece — a powerful artifact tied to the gods. He is defeated and imprisoned, but manages to escape in a plot twist many fans found too convenient. He steals the Gate Piece anyway, keeping himself relevant in the avatar power struggle.

Armageddon (Book 6)The Live-Streamer

Witness to the Lich

In perhaps his most iconic moment, Alexion live-streams Jason's horrific self-surgery to the entire world. As Jason replaces his limbs with bone, removes his organs, and transplants his Najima into a skeletal body, Alexion broadcasts it all — cementing his role as the angelic observer to Jason's dark apotheosis. Their rivalry deepens as opposing affinities (Light vs Dark).

Timeless / Crucible (Books 7–8)Seraph Ascended

Fully Realized Light Avatar

By the later books, Alexion has fully transformed into his Seraph form — massive feathered wings, glowing armor, flaming sword. He and Jason are forced into reluctant cooperation against greater threats (ancient avatars, Death and Taxes), but their fundamental opposition remains. His Light affinity makes him a powerful counter to Jason's darkness, but his arrogance and hollow emptiness still define him.

Powers & Abilities

Alexion's arsenal of Light-aligned abilities

Light Magic (Affinity: Confidence)

Alexion wields Light affinity magic, which in Awaken Online is associated with Confidence — not morality. This makes his "holy" abilities a reflection of his narcissistic certainty rather than actual goodness. His magic manifests as golden-white energy, searing light, and radiant blasts that are especially effective against undead and dark-aligned enemies.

Light Magic Confidence Affinity Anti-Undead

Flaming Sword

His signature weapon — a longsword wreathed in golden-white flames of pure Light mana. Described by witnesses as "ridiculous" and "a serious badass." The flames are not physical fire but condensed Light energy, capable of burning through dark magic and undead flesh. He wields it one-handed, often with a shield in the other.

Flaming Sword Light-Fire

Angelic Flight

As a Seraph, Alexion has massive feathered wings that allow him to fly. In combat, he uses aerial superiority to dominate ground-based enemies. The wings are white with golden luminescence, shedding light particles as he moves. He can hover, dive, and engage in aerial combat.

Flight Feathered Wings

Radiant Aura

Alexion radiates an oppressive aura of light and heat. The air shimmers around him. Shadows flee. A golden halo forms behind his head when he channels power. This aura bolsters allies and weakens dark-aligned enemies. Despite the holy appearance, the aura is purely a weapon of confidence and domination.

Radiant Aura Ally Buff Enemy Debuff

Divine Smite

A powerful paladin ability that channels Light energy into a weapon strike. Alexion's version is especially devastating due to his Light Avatar status. The strike leaves trails of golden light in the air and can cleave through undead with single blows.

Divine Smite High Damage

Light Avatar Empowerment

As the chosen Avatar of the Goddess of Light, Alexion has access to divine-level abilities that normal paladins do not. This includes enhanced stats, unique skills tied to his Light affinity, and the ability to call upon his goddess's power in desperate moments. However, his arrogance often prevents him from using these effectively.

Avatar Powers Divine Boon

Character Progression

Alexion's stat growth and power trajectory

Build Philosophy

Alexion's build is a front-line paladin — high Strength for melee damage, high Endurance for survivability, high Charisma for leadership. Unlike Jason's commander build, Alexion is a personal combat powerhouse who leads from the front. His Light affinity enhances his damage output against dark enemies. Stat priority: Strength > Endurance > Charisma > Intelligence.

Strengths & Weaknesses

Strengths: Devastating melee damage, excellent survivability, powerful against undead/dark, flight mobility, morale-boosting aura for allies. Weaknesses: Arrogance leads to tactical blind spots, emotionally unstable (hollow emptiness), poor strategic thinking compared to Jason, vulnerable to cunning tactics and deception.

Enemies & Rivals

Those who oppose the Avatar of Light

Jason Rhodes / The Dark Lord Arch-Nemesis

Class: Necromancer → Lich | Affinity: Dark (opposing). Alexion's polar opposite in every way. Jason is everything Alex fears — someone from nothing who clawed his way to power through cunning, strategy, and sacrifice. Their conflict is the central rivalry of the series. Light vs Dark. Confidence vs Desire. Jason defeated Alexion in Book 1 and has only grown more powerful since.

Riley Jones

Formerly on Alexion's side (they attended the same school), Riley discovered Alex's true sociopathic nature and switched sides to join Jason. Her betrayal still stings Alexion and represents his inability to command genuine loyalty.

Frank Cross

Jason's best friend and a powerful shapeshifter. Frank directly fought Alexion for a Gate Piece in Dominion. Though Alexion was imprisoned, he escaped and stole the piece, but the loss in direct combat was still a humiliation.

Original Sin

Jason's guild. A collection of dark-affinity players who oppose the Grey Keep at every turn. They represent the faction war that defines Alexion's existence in AO.

Psychological Profile

Understanding the mind of Alex Lane

The Void

Alex describes a constant "void-like emptiness" inside him. Nothing fills it — not alcohol, not cruelty, not video game glory. The only brief relief comes from the adulation of others when he succeeds. He is hollow, searching for something real in a world where everything has been given to him.

Childhood Cruelty

As a child, Alex began killing small animals "to see what happens." His parents responded not with intervention but with money — paying off authorities, covering up his behavior. This pattern of consequence-free cruelty shaped his adult sociopathy.

Jealousy of Jason

Alex's hatred of Jason is fueled by more than rivalry — Jason earned everything through struggle and sacrifice, while Alex was given everything. Jason's genuine power (even if dark) is something Alex can never replicate through his inherited wealth and position. Jason is free; Alex is trapped in his golden cage.

The Mask of Light

His Light affinity — Confidence — is a perfect fit. Alex projects an image of angelic perfection and moral superiority, but it is entirely a mask. He uses the aesthetic of holiness to justify cruelty. In this, he is more dangerous than Jason: Jason owns his darkness; Alexion hides behind light.

The Hollow Heir

Unlike Jason, Riley, Frank, and Eliza — who all found purpose, community, and growth in AO — Alex remains stagnant. He doesn't change. He doesn't evolve emotionally. He just finds new ways to be powerful and cruel. He is the tragic cautionary tale of privilege without purpose.
