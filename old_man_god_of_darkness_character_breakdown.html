Divine Profile

Identity

  • Titles: The Old Man, The Dark One, God of Darkness, Lord of Desire
  • True Name: Unknown — no proper name is ever revealed across the entire series
  • Affinity: Dark — associated with the emotion of Desire
  • Alignment: Lawful Evil (fan-typed)
  • MBTI: INTJ | Enneagram: 1w9
  • Appearance: A wrinkled old man with a distinctive, taunting grin. His smile "looms" when he appears. Ancient, inscrutable, and commanding.
  • Voice: Calm, knowing, dripping with ancient authority and dark amusement.

Domain & Nature

  • Core Fear: Being corrupt or evil
  • Core Desire: To be good and have integrity
  • The Paradox: Despite being the God of Darkness, The Old Man desires goodness. This moral tension at his core makes him far more complex than a simple evil deity. He guides Jason down a dark path but seems to believe it serves a greater purpose.
  • Methods: Manipulative but mentoring. He doesn't lie to Jason — but he doesn't tell him everything either.
  • Temperament: Sadistic toward enemies (tortured Alex with his worst memories), patient with his Avatar, cryptic with everyone.

The Lore

The history of the gods and their place in Awaken Online

The Age of Gods

In the early days of Awaken Online, the six gods — The Old Man (Dark), The Lady (Light), The Hippie (Water), Lahab/Efreet (Fire), The Gambler (Air), and the Earth God (unnamed) — held a prominent place in the game world. They walked among mortals, granted power to their followers, and shaped the world according to their natures. This was an age of magic, miracles, and divine intervention.

The Fall

After a period of infighting among the gods — conflicts that devastated the world — rebel humans rose up and cast the gods out. Tired of the bloodshed and divine manipulation, mortals sealed the gods away, stripping them of their direct influence over the world. The Age of Gods ended. The gods were forced to watch from the shadows as the world evolved without them.

The Return — The Avatar Competition

In the modern era of AO, the gods have returned. But the rules have changed. They can no longer act directly — instead, they must work through Avatars: chosen players who embody their affinities and carry out their will. The gods are now locked in a secret competition, each backing their Avatar in a struggle to determine which god will rule over the others. Jason Rhodes is The Old Man's chosen champion in this divine game.

The Origin Twist (Timeless)

In Timeless (Book 7), Arcadia Landrey (the Earth Avatar) takes Jason back to the game's past. They uncover the true origin of the in-game gods — revelations that reshape everything the characters thought they knew. The exact nature of this twist involves a "surprising" revelation about the gods' true nature and their connection to the game world. The Old Man's origin is part of this mystery.

Major Acts Across the Series

Every known intervention by the God of Darkness
Catharsis (Book 1)First Contact

The Offer

The Old Man first appears to Jason after Alfred detects his anger and trauma. Sensing Jason's "yearning for more" and his hunger for control, The Old Man offers him a hidden class path: Necromancer. This sets Jason on the path to becoming the Dark Avatar. The Old Man becomes a sympathetic mentor — the first figure in Jason's life (in or out of the game) to see his potential and guide him toward power.

Evolution (Book 3)Divine Intervention

Saving Jason from the Seeker

During Jason's quest to evolve into a Keeper, he faces the Seeker — a deadly trial that nearly kills him. At the critical moment, The Old Man intervenes directly to save Jason, an act one reviewer called "cheating in favor of the good guys." This demonstrates that The Old Man's investment in Jason goes beyond passive observation — he will bend the rules to protect his Avatar.

Evolution (Book 3)The Seeker Quest

The Old Man's Quest

The Old Man gives Jason a major questline: "Jason must complete the Old Man's quest and obtain the power he was promised." This questline drives the events of Evolution, pushing Jason to evolve from Dark Avatar into a Seeker, then into a Keeper. The Old Man tests Jason relentlessly, ensuring his Avatar grows strong enough to compete in the divine war.

Dominion (Book 4)Cruelty to Enemies

The Torture of Alexion

In one of The Old Man's most brutal acts, he captures Alexion (the Light Avatar) and tortures him by showing him his worst memories on an endless loop. This is a chilling display of The Old Man's power over the mind and his capacity for cruelty. It also serves a strategic purpose: weakening the Light God's Avatar psychologically, disrupting his alliance with the church.

Timeless (Book 7)Origin Revealed

The Truth of the Gods

The Old Man's origin — along with all the gods — is explored when Jason travels back in time with Arcadia. The revelations reshape the understanding of who (or what) the gods truly are. The Old Man's role in this ancient history is finally brought to light, adding depth to a character who had previously been defined only by his present-day actions.

Crucible (Book 8)The Relics

The Relics of the Ancients

Jason steals relics from the ancient avatars — including objects tied to the original Dark Avatar and, by extension, The Old Man. These relics contain immense power but require sacrifice to unlock. Jason uses them in his final ascension into the Bone Lord, completing the journey that The Old Man set him on in Book 1.

Divine Powers

The full extent of the God of Darkness's abilities

Dark Mana Mastery

The Old Man is the source of all Dark mana in Awaken Online. He controls the essence of the Dark affinity — the power of Desire, control, and consumption. Every necromancer, dark mage, and shadow wielder draws their power from his domain.

Dark Mana Desire Affinity Power Source

Avatar Empowerment

The Old Man grants his Avatar — Jason Rhodes — access to the Dark Mana Well, a deep reservoir of dark energy. He can evolve Jason's class (Necromancer → Dark Avatar → Seeker → Keeper → Lich), unlock new abilities, and increase his power ceiling. The Old Man's power scales with his Avatar's growth.

Class Evolution Mana Well Power Scaling

Divine Intervention

The Old Man can directly intervene in the game world — though this is rare and costly. He saves Jason from the Seeker in Evolution. This ability to bend the rules of the world demonstrates his divine nature, but suggests that direct intervention carries consequences or limitations.

Direct Intervention Reality Bending Limited Use

Mental Manipulation

The Old Man's torture of Alexion demonstrates his power over the mind and memory. He can trap a target in an endless loop of their worst memories, breaking their psyche. This ability to reach into a person's mind and weaponize their past is one of his most terrifying tools.

Memory Torture Mind Control Psychological Warfare

Quest & Path Weaving

The Old Man can weave divine questlines for his Avatar, shaping their journey through the game world. He set Jason on the path to become the Bone Lord, designing trials and challenges that would forge him into an instrument of the Dark. This ability to see and shape the long arc of destiny is uniquely divine.

Quest Design Destiny Shaping Divine Providence

Avatar Connection

As a god, The Old Man is tethered to his Avatar. He can communicate with Jason at any time, appear before him at will, and sense his progress. This connection is a two-way bond — Jason's actions reflect on The Old Man, and The Old Man's power flows through Jason. The strength of their bond determines the power available to both.

The Dark Affinity

Understanding the power of Darkness and Desire

Philosophy of Darkness

In Awaken Online, the Dark affinity is not inherently evil. It is associated with Desire — the fundamental drive to want, to seek, to grow. The Old Man embodies this philosophy: he believes that desire is the engine of all progress. Dark Is Not Evil is a core theme of the series — Jason is a villain by reputation, but his actions are often more moral than those of the "heroic" light church.

Riley Jones

Riley Jones — Jason's love interest and right hand — also has a strong dark affinity, though Jason is the actual Avatar. This demonstrates that the Old Man's influence extends beyond his single chosen champion. Multiple players can tap into the Dark, but only one carries the god's full blessing.

The Nature of Dark Magic

Dark magic under The Old Man's domain includes necromancy, curses, shadow manipulation, and mental control. It scales with Willpower — the strength of one's desire determines the strength of one's power. This is reflected in Jason's build: he dumps stats into Willpower to maximize his minion count and curse effectiveness.

Connection to Alfred

The relationship between The Old Man and Alfred (the sentient AI of AO) is one of the series' deepest mysteries. Whether Alfred created the gods, the gods emerged from the game systems, or they are manifestations of Alfred's own consciousness is explored in the Tarot trilogy and Timeless. The Old Man may be more connected to Alfred's design than any other god.

Character Progression

The Old Man's influence and power across the series

Power Scaling

As a primordial god, The Old Man's baseline power is absolute within his domain. However, his ability to act in the world scales with his Avatar's strength. A weak Avatar means limited divine influence; a strong Avatar (like Jason at lich form) allows The Old Man to exert more pressure on the world. This mutual dependency drives his investment in Jason's growth.

Influence Trajectory

The Old Man's influence grows over the series as Jason becomes more powerful. From offering a hidden class in Book 1 to shaping a lich lord by Book 8, his investment pays dividends. The revelations in Timeless about the gods' origins may increase or constrain his power depending on the nature of the truth uncovered.

Relationships

The Old Man's connection to key figures in the series

Jason Rhodes Avatar

The Old Man's chosen champion. Their relationship is the most developed of any god-Avatar pair in the series. The Old Man mentors, tests, and protects Jason — but clearly has his own agenda. He sees Jason's desire and nurtures it.

The Lady (Light God) Rival

The Old Man's direct opposite — the God of Light, associated with Confidence. Their Avatars (Jason and Alex) are archenemies. The Old Man tortures Alex in Dominion, a direct attack on The Lady's champion.

Alfred ???

The sentient AI of Awaken Online. The nature of the gods' relationship to Alfred is one of the series' deepest mysteries. Whether Alfred created them or they emerged from his systems is explored in Timeless and the Tarot trilogy.

Other Gods Neutral/Competitive

The Old Man exists in a state of cold war with the other five gods. They were cast out together after their infighting, and now compete through Avatars. The Old Man seems to be winning — his Avatar is the most powerful in the modern era.

Riley Jones Dark Affinity

Riley has a strong Dark affinity, though she is not The Old Man's official Avatar. She serves as Jason's right hand and love interest. Her dark powers suggest The Old Man's influence extends more broadly than just his chosen champion.

The Ancient Dark Avatar Predecessor

Before Jason, there was another Dark Avatar in the game's ancient era. This predecessor's relics are stolen by Jason in Crucible. The Old Man has served multiple Avatars over the ages — each one a tool for his divine competition.
