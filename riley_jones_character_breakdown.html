Character Overview

Real Life — Riley Marie Jones

  • Age: 18 at series start
  • Appearance: Blonde hair, petite frame, described as having an almost glowing energy and confidence. Kind face. "Not the prettiest girl at school" but has an effervescent quality that draws people in.
  • School: Richmond Private School. Senior. Member of the lacrosse team.
  • Family: Wealthy (attends Richmond), but unlike Alex, she turned out decent.
  • Personality: Kindest person at school. Was always nice to Jason despite his outcast status. But she has a breaking point — when pushed, rage and fury emerge. She struggles with trusting people after Alex's betrayal.
  • Relationship with Alex: Was dating him before discovering his true sociopathic nature. Alex blackmailed her with embarrassing photos/videos to stay with him. After she confronted and left him, the girls at school tormented her on his behalf.

In Game — Riley Jones

  • In-Game Name: Riley (same)
  • Class: Archer (Dark Affinity archer)
  • Alignmenbt: Lawful Evil
  • Affinity: Dark — Desire
  • Faction: Original Sin / Twilight Throne
  • Role: Jason's right hand, close friend, later girlfriend
  • Signature Weapon: A mysterious bow discovered in a dungeon north of Peccavi. The bow belonged to a woman named Lily, who was killed by cultists. Carries a quest of vengeance.
  • Signature Moment: During the final battle of Book 1, when Alexion was gloating over his victory, Riley approached from behind and beheaded him — ending the war and making Alexion a laughingstock across all of AO.

The Transformation

Riley's journey from Alex's pawn to Jason's most trusted ally — and her own hero
Catharsis (Book 1)The Switch

Breaking Free of Alex

Riley starts the series as Alex's girlfriend — but she's not like him. When she discovers his true sociopathic nature (he blackmails her with compromising photos to keep her from leaving), she makes the courageous choice to switch sides. She begins helping Jason and becomes his closest ally in-game. Her defining moment: beheading the gloating Alexion on the battlefield, turning the tide of the war.

Precipice (Book 2)The Bow

Discovery of the Mysterious Bow

Riley explores a dungeon north of Peccavi and discovers a strange, powerful bow with a dark history. The bow belonged to a woman named Lily, who was killed by cultists. The weapon carries a lingering quest for vengeance. Its discovery sets Riley on a path that will define her solo journey.

Retribution (Book 2.5)Protagonist — Her Own Story

The Quest for Vengeance

With Jason mysteriously disappeared (arrested), Riley takes center stage in her own side quest. She investigates the bow's origin and travels to Vaerwald, a floating mage city divided into twelve levels — each representing a social tier. She teams with Ethan, Lucas, and the jealous light mage Emma. The city is plagued by a magical plague linked to cultists. Riley must avenge Lily's death and decide the fate of the entire city. She struggles with her rage, often fueled by real-world frustrations, and must learn to control her fury to succeed.

Evolution + Beyond (Books 3+)The Dark Archer

Right Hand of the Dark Lord

Riley returns to the main storyline as Jason's trusted lieutenant and girlfriend. She fights alongside him against Thorn, Finn, Death and Taxes, and the ancient avatars. Her Dark affinity grows stronger. She has moments of intense anger against enemies that unlock new powers. Like Jason, she finds strength through her rage, but struggles with the consequences of her violent outbursts in the hyper-realistic game world.

Powers & Abilities

Riley's arsenal as a Dark-affinity archer

Dark Archery

Riley is a highly skilled archer with a Dark affinity bow. Her arrows are infused with Dark mana, leaving trails of purple-black shadow in flight. She can imbue her shots with various effects — increased damage, homing, or explosive shadow bursts. Her accuracy is exceptional, capable of hitting vital points from extreme range.

Dark Arrows Precision Shots Shadow Trails

Wrath-Fueled Power

Riley's intense anger and fury against enemies often unlocks new abilities mid-combat. When she taps into her rage — often triggered by enemies that remind her of real-world trauma — her Dark affinity surges, her eyes glow purple, and her arrows hit with devastating force. This is both a strength and a struggle, as she sometimes loses control.

Rage-Fueled Power Surge

Dark Affinity Magic

Like Jason, Riley has a strong Dark affinity (Desire). While she is primarily an archer, her Dark mana enhances her abilities — she can sense darkness in the environment, track targets through shadow, and her arrows carry Dark energy that corrupts and weakens enemies. Her Dark affinity also gives her resistance to Light-based attacks.

Dark Sense Shadow Tracking

Leadership & Loyalty

Riley is Jason's right hand in the Twilight Throne. She commands respect through loyalty and competence, not fear. She manages the city alongside the guild Original Sin. Her real-world experience standing up to Alex and enduring the subsequent bullying gave her a resilience that makes her invaluable in crisis situations.

Character Progression

Riley's growth across the series

Build Philosophy

Riley is a Dexterity-based archer with points in Perception for ranged accuracy. She also invests in Willpower for Dark mana reserves and Intellect for ability damage scaling. Unlike Jason's commander build, she is a precision striker — eliminating high-value targets from range. Stat priority: Dexterity > Perception > Willpower > Intellect.

Character Arc

Riley's progression is as much emotional as it is mechanical. She grows from a victim of Alex's manipulation to a confident leader in her own right. Her solo book (Retribution) marks her full emergence as a protagonist. She learns to channel her rage without being consumed by it. Her relationship with Jason becomes a source of strength for both of them.

Psychological Profile

Understanding Riley's inner world

The Mask of Kindness

Riley is universally described as the kindest person at Richmond. She was nice to Jason when no one else was. But this kindness is a mask she wears to survive. Beneath it is a deep well of fury and pain — from Alex's betrayal, the bullying, and the pressure to maintain appearances.

The Rage Within

When Riley fights, her anger surfaces violently. She has moments of intense fury against NPCs that are often fueled by real-world trauma. These rage episodes unlock new powers but also frighten her. She struggles with the gore and violence of AO, even as she wields it effectively.

Trust Issues

Alex's blackmail and manipulation left deep scars. Riley is wary of others and small breaches of trust hurt her more than they should. Her journey in Retribution forces her to trust new companions despite her instincts. Her relationship with Jason helps heal this wound.

Finding Her Own Power

Riley's arc is about finding agency. She starts as Alex's girlfriend, then becomes Jason's ally, then becomes her own hero in Retribution. By the later books, she is a leader in her own right — no longer defined by the men around her. AO gave her the strength to stand up for herself in real life.

Real-World Victory

At the end of Retribution, Riley faces the girls Alex sent to torment her in real life and defeats them decisively. She brings her in-game confidence into the real world — a mirror of Jason's own journey. AO didn't just make her powerful in a game; it gave her the strength to reclaim her real life.
